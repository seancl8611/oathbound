# Akio

Akio is a disciplined swordsman of the Order and the player character of Oathbound.

## Character fantasy

A functional, travel-worn warrior who carries steel, ritual tools, and a contained supernatural danger. He is quietly capable rather than noble, theatrical, or heroic.

## Defining traits

- Strong resolve and disciplined purpose
- Trained in katana combat, parries, posture pressure, and execution
- Uses ritual prosthetic tools
- Carries Returning Blood after forcing Beast Blood to submit
- Returns after death without losing his human identity
- Can shape Returning Blood into Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin Blood Aspects

## Visual direction

- Layered working-warrior clothing rather than heavy plate
- Wrapped limbs and restrained ritual marks
- Compact grounded stance
- Clear katana silhouette and attack arcs
- Small functional indication of prosthetic storage
- Mutation should be modular and progressive, not twelve replacement character sheets

## Narrative role

Akio is a counterpoint to the Eclipse Shogun. Both command Beast Blood, but Akio's control is based on disciplined acceptance of death and return rather than permanent preservation of others.

## Canon restrictions

- Akio is not naturally immune.
- His Returning Blood is not routine Order practice.
- Blood Aspects are controlled mutations of Returning Blood, not unrelated magical classes.
