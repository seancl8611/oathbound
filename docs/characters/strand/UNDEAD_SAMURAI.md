# The Undead Samurai

## Gameplay and narrative role

The Undead Samurai is the Blood Cavern's ancient master of instruction. He teaches fundamentals, provides advanced trials, and tests mastery. He is a living repository of the Order's martial discipline and evidence that the struggle has lasted far longer than the current generation understands.

## One-sentence fantasy

An undying swordsman of the old Order, preserved by discipline and corruption alike, who trains each new warrior because duty is the only thing left in him that still lives.

## Visual identity

A withered archaic samurai preserved in old Order armor, standing with perfect form despite obvious physical death. He should show:

- hollow, dried features,
- ritual wrappings,
- old scars,
- dim ember- or blood-lit eyes,
- cracked lacquer and faded cloth,
- brittle cords and old insignia,
- a blade kept in disciplined condition.

He must not read as a feral zombie, skeleton, or generic undead horror. The key is martial dignity under impossible preservation.

## Silhouette

Upright, centered, formal, and severe:

- straight spine,
- balanced stance,
- visible sword,
- layered armor plates,
- controlled shoulder shape,
- long tabards or cloth elements reinforcing an older Order identity.

Even decay should not break the clean instructor silhouette.

## Personality in motion

He moves like a master whose habits outlived his humanity:

- precise turns,
- measured steps,
- perfect weapon handling,
- calm resets to stance,
- no shuffle, stagger, or wasted motion.

His unsettling quality comes from how correct he remains.

## Idle and interaction needs

Required states:

- formal near-motionless idle,
- slight grip adjustment,
- minimal stance change,
- slow draw-check/reseat motion,
- interaction acknowledgment,
- restrained instruct/talk loop,
- stance demonstration,
- corrective hand or sheathed-blade gesture,
- challenge/trial-start posture,
- optional short sword-form beats.

Praise should be conveyed through reduced correction or slight acceptance rather than overt approval.

## Technical notes

- Prioritize posture, sword placement, armor layering, and head/face contrast.
- Keep supporting cavern resonance and red reflection separate from the body where possible.
- If used in sparring or demonstrations, his motion must emphasize mastery over spectacle.
- Thematic emphasis is discipline surviving corruption.
