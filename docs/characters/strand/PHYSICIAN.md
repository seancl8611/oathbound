# The Physician

## Role and arc

A major Strand NPC and Order-affiliated physician/ritual researcher who fits Akio with a first-field-test protective binding intended to preserve humanity against Beast Blood. He does **not** knowingly create a resurrection device.

When Akio unexpectedly returns, the physician is relieved but troubled. The binding has changed and appears to adapt alongside the curse. Over subsequent runs he studies Akio's condition; his fascination and concern coexist. Whether it becomes inseparable, fails, or can be removed remains unresolved. He is not automatically a villain.

## Hub service

He provides the narrative research/medical framing of the **existing Bloodwell Akio + Run Infrastructure** progression. There is no fourth upgrade station, new currency or independent skill tree. The Scribe owns archives, discoveries and Akio's personal bond; the Smith owns Prosthetics and Relics.

## Working dialogue (not finalized)

Before departure: "The binding should resist corruption. How long it will hold, I cannot say."

First return: "You're alive... The binding held. But it was never designed to bring you back."

Later: "The changes are no longer confined to your blood. The binding itself is adapting."

## Visual/production intent

Restrained Japanese-gothic physician in protective robes/apron, gloves, medical tools, ritual seals, samples and a compact workbench near the Bloodwell. Distinct from the Smith's forge and Scribe's records. Idles may include inspecting samples, adjusting a binding and writing notes.
