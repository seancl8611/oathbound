# The Raven

## Gameplay and narrative role

The Raven is the Order's courier for vows, notices, quest progress, recovered rewards, and tokens. It connects the Strand to ongoing obligations and represents duty that outlives the individual warriors sent toward the island.

## One-sentence fantasy

A blood-marked war raven of the Order that carries memory, duty, and the weight of the dead from one failed crossing to the next.

## Visual identity

A large, old, intelligent raven with a weathered working presence rather than a cute or mystical design. It remains mostly naturalistic but carries practical signs of long Order service:

- ritual cords,
- small tags and seals,
- token loops,
- message tube,
- worn harness elements.

Blood influence should stay subtle: irregular feather discoloration, wrong sheen, old ritual bands, or scar-like markings near the legs, chest, or eyes.

## Silhouette

Sharp, iconic, and readable as a hardened courier bird:

- strong perched profile,
- slightly oversized intelligent head shape,
- layered neck and shoulder feathers,
- long tapered wings,
- visible but uncluttered harness/tube shapes.

Its perch or post should be treated as part of the full presentation.

## Personality in motion

The Raven behaves like a distrustful but dependable veteran:

- quick assessing head turns,
- deliberate hops,
- powerful wing adjustments,
- total stillness while observing,
- grudging recognition rather than soft affection.

Familiarity may increase over time, but the bird should retain animal severity.

## Idle and interaction needs

Required states:

- perch idle,
- alert variant,
- claw adjustment,
- feather ruffle or wing tuck,
- beak wipe,
- interaction acknowledgment,
- message/token presentation,
- arrival and landing,
- takeoff and departure.

It may cock its head, step closer, stare, or issue a clipped call when delivering important information.

## Technical notes

- Design for top-down/three-quarter readability first.
- Keep accessories modular and legible at game scale.
- The Raven is a practical Order animal, not a mascot, comic-relief character, or overt magical familiar.
- Thematic emphasis is memory and unfinished duty.
