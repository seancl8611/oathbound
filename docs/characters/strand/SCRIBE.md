# The Scribe

## Gameplay and narrative role

The Scribe is the Strand's archivist, recorder, and keeper of practical knowledge. She maintains the Discovery Board and catalogs enemy weaknesses, prosthetic discoveries, relic notes, and fragments of island history. She supports codex progression, discovery tracking, and the gradual conversion of recovered knowledge into useful understanding.

## One-sentence fantasy

A gentle but guarded young Order scribe who preserves the island's truths in careful records, offering knowledge, clarity, and hard-won understanding at the edge of ruin.

## Visual identity

A young woman in layered, weathered recorder's robes suited to the Strand's cold wind and long working nights. Her design should feel scholarly, practical, and quietly human rather than noble or ceremonial:

- soft dark fabrics and faded wraps,
- layered outer robe or cloak,
- belts, pouches, tags, cords, scroll cases, brushes, and writing tools,
- journal, ledger, or record bundles.

She should feel warm and approachable without appearing fully open; restraint and guarded knowledge remain part of the presentation.

## Silhouette

Slim, softly vertical, and immediately scholarly:

- long draped outer layer,
- book or ledger as a strong prop,
- hanging tags and writing tools,
- light waist-hung record materials,
- clear head-and-shoulder separation.

She should read as a record-keeper, not a merchant, fighter, or shrine maiden.

## Personality in motion

Calm, kind, attentive, and precise. She handles paper and tools carefully, thinks before speaking, and reveals guardedness through small hesitations, measured eye contact, and controlled stillness rather than dramatic secrecy.

## Idle and interaction needs

Required states:

- standing or seated record idle,
- reading and writing variants,
- page turn,
- paper sorting and tying,
- board-note adjustment,
- thoughtful pause,
- interaction acknowledgment,
- restrained talk loop,
- gesture toward Discovery Board,
- optional Raven-care or message-tie ambient beat.

Routine discoveries should feel practical. Deeper truths may use a lowered gaze, pause, or reduced motion to show trust being measured.

## Technical notes

- Prioritize clean robe layering and readable book, brush, scroll, and tag props.
- The Discovery Board and study materials are part of her character presentation.
- Use shape grouping rather than tiny detail.
- Keep her grounded and practical instead of mystical.
- Thematic emphasis is preserved knowledge, gentle competence, and guarded trust.
