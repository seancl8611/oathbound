# First Attempt

The first attempt begins at the Strand and follows **normal Hushiro Floor 1 gameplay**, not a mandatory Shogun prologue. Akio starts fully human and uninfected but wears an experimental protective binding from the Order physician.

# Opening structure

1. A short Strand preparation sequence introduces the physician and the protective binding.
2. Akio crosses by Boat into Hushiro, starting at the normal first chamber.
3. Early in the route, an authored serious blood-bearing wound directly introduces Beast Blood despite incomplete protection. Ordinary proximity and incidental scratches are not universal transmission.
4. The device changes as it partly restrains corruption. Akio continues with his ordinary sword kit and no active Blood Aspect.
5. His **first genuine death**, wherever it occurs, reveals unexpected Returning Blood reconstruction at the Strand.
6. The physician examines the device, the Scribe records his return, and the normal post-awakening loop opens.

A mastery player can survive farther into the route, including to the Shogun. Before awakening, **no Heart Binding may be broken**. Exceptional Heart contact may become a first-death awakening endpoint without granting Binding progress. Exact edge-case encounter choreography remains implementation work.

# First-attempt loadout

Akio has his approved human sword kit and appropriate starter tools. He has no Blood Aspect, Corruption/Tier growth, Blood, Blood Art, or permanent Bloodwell/Blood Mirror progression before first return. The binding is protective, not a Blood weapon.

# Direct exposure and death

The first exposure is an intentional early narrative encounter using a blood-bearing wound, not environmental contagion. The physician intended protection of humanity, not immortality. The binding begins adapting to the curse. On the first genuine death, Beast Blood rebuilds Akio while the altered device helps preserve his identity.

The Strand is Akio's subconscious safe destination. No talisman tether or Bloodwell ritual summons him.

# Transition

After awakening, existing Blood Aspect selection, Bloodwell and Mirror unlocks, normal route structure, six remaining Bindings, six successful post-awakening clears, final Heart confrontation and postgame remain intact.

# Implementation note

This revision locks **narrative canon**. Godot first-attempt scripts and smoke tests may still use earlier assumptions and require a separate implementation change.
