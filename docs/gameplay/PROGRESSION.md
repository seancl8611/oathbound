# Progression

Oathbound uses two connected progression layers.

## Run progression

Temporary power assembled during an attempt may include:

- Blood Aspect Tier growth
- Boons and relic effects
- Stance access or enhancements
- Prosthetic resources or modifications
- Consumables and temporary materials
- Area and boss rewards

## Persistent progression

The Strand supports progression that survives death. Current categories include:

- Forge upgrades
- Bloodwell upgrades
- Discoveries and codex knowledge
- New tools, options, and systems
- NPC relationship or service progression
- Blood Mirror trials or challenge unlocks

## Design rules

- Persistent growth may improve options and resilience, but should not erase the need to read combat.
- Run growth should create meaningful build identity before the final area.
- Unlocks should be documented separately from balance values.
- Every persistent system must state what is saved, what resets, and what is lost on death.
