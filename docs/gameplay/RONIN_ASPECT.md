# Ronin Blood Aspect

## Status

This document owns Ronin's approved qualitative Tier 0 weapon kit for roster comparison.

It rejects the earlier directions based on preserving a combo through defense, playing around reaching Judgment Stroke, or selecting basic attacks through movement-direction input.

It does not approve exact numerical values, frame data, hitboxes, animation counts, Blood Art, Tier progression, drawbacks, Corruption interactions, production scope, or Ronin's final inclusion after the roster audit.

## Core weapon identity

**Ronin is the slow, precise, heavy-hitting kit.**

Its swordplay is defined by:

- a three-hit heavy basic sequence,
- conventional medium sword reach,
- the slowest basic cadence of the initial three,
- the highest per-hit health damage,
- the highest or near-highest per-hit enemy-posture pressure,
- strong ordinary-enemy stagger,
- minimal movement during heavy attacks,
- restrained tracking after commitment,
- a defining high-damage Held Attack,
- and severe punishment after missed major strikes.

Ronin is not built around maintaining a combo. Each basic attack is valuable on its own, and the player may stop after any hit.

The player-facing difference is direct and concrete:

> Ronin attacks less often, but each clean strike removes more health, applies more posture pressure, and creates stronger impact than the equivalent Wolf or Wraith hit.

## Universal systems retained

Ronin uses the universal:

- ordinary movement speed,
- neutral dash distance, speed, startup, invulnerability, recovery, steering, and repeat availability,
- defense input,
- parry timing and success conditions,
- posture-break and deathblow rules,
- Technique system,
- and prosthetic system.

Ronin's heavy feel comes from its attacks. Akio does not run or dash more slowly while this Aspect is equipped.

## Basic attack sequence

Repeated Basic Attack presses produce:

1. **Severing Cut**
2. **Crushing Cross**
3. **Bloodfall**

The sequence escalates from a practical heavy opener to a broader heavy follow-up and then the most committed basic strike.

The player is not required to reach Bloodfall. A small opening may justify only Severing Cut, while a larger opening may permit two or all three attacks.

### Severing Cut

Severing Cut is Ronin's practical heavy opener.

Working properties:

- medium sword reach,
- slower startup than Wolf's or Wraith's opener,
- limited forward movement,
- a strong diagonal or horizontal arc,
- high health damage,
- strong enemy-posture damage,
- noticeable stagger against ordinary enemies,
- moderate recovery after contact,
- and heavy recovery after a miss.

Primary uses:

- punish an ordinary opening,
- interrupt a weaker enemy when the hit lands first,
- remove a meaningful portion of an ordinary enemy's health,
- apply a large posture chunk to an elite or boss,
- and begin Ronin's optional heavier follow-ups.

Severing Cut must remain practical enough to use in normal combat. Ronin should not require a boss-sized opening for its first basic attack.

### Crushing Cross

Crushing Cross is a heavy returning or horizontal slash.

Working properties:

- broader frontal coverage than Severing Cut,
- high health damage,
- strong ordinary-enemy stagger,
- strong enemy-posture pressure,
- enough side coverage to hit nearby enemies in front of Akio,
- minimal forward movement,
- low-to-moderate tracking,
- and longer recovery than Severing Cut.

Primary uses:

- continue a larger punish opportunity,
- strike multiple enemies grouped in front of Ronin,
- heavily damage the current target,
- and create a forceful second beat before deciding whether Bloodfall is safe.

Crushing Cross gains breadth through a forceful sword arc, not through Wraith's long spectral reach.

### Bloodfall

Bloodfall is Ronin's most committed basic attack: a steep diagonal or vertical Blood-reinforced strike.

Working properties:

- Ronin's highest basic-attack health damage,
- extremely strong enemy-posture damage,
- heavy frontal impact,
- strong ordinary-enemy stagger,
- limited nearby coverage,
- very low tracking after commitment,
- little or no forward movement,
- and severe recovery after a miss.

Primary uses:

- punish a large opening,
- finish an ordinary enemy,
- stagger a durable enemy,
- heavily damage an elite,
- and drive a boss toward posture break.

Bloodfall is not the objective of the Aspect. Reaching it does not grant a combo bonus, and the player should not use it when the opening is too small.

## Sequence flow

Ronin's basic sequence is:

> Severing Cut → Crushing Cross → Bloodfall

The player may stop after any attack and return to:

- movement,
- block,
- parry,
- universal dash,
- Prosthetic use,
- or another available action.

There is no combo meter, sequence-preservation rule, completion bonus, or special restart.

The sequence simply offers increasing commitment:

- Severing Cut fits ordinary openings.
- Crushing Cross fits a larger opening or grouped frontal targets.
- Bloodfall fits the largest safe openings.

## Why Ronin uses three attacks

Three attacks give Ronin enough space to express escalating weight without creating Wolf's long pressure string.

The approved structure is:

> heavy practical strike → broad heavy strike → decisive maximum-commitment strike

A two-hit sequence would compress too much of Ronin's heavy progression into one follow-up. A four-hit sequence would undermine the slow, high-impact rhythm and overlap with Wolf.

## Held Attack — Stillness Draw

Holding and releasing Attack produces **Stillness Draw**.

Returning Blood forms a dense spectral sheath around the physical katana before Ronin releases a powerful draw cut.

Working properties:

- medium-to-long effective reach,
- shorter reach than Wraith's Pale Lance,
- Ronin's highest single-hit health damage,
- extremely strong enemy-posture pressure,
- major ordinary-enemy stagger,
- a strong frontal or diagonal attack line,
- minimal movement,
- very limited tracking after release,
- vulnerable preparation,
- and severe recovery after a miss.

Primary uses:

- punish a boss's major recovery,
- intercept a predictable enemy approach,
- heavily damage an elite,
- open combat against an unaware enemy,
- and capitalize on an opening created by a Prosthetic or another combat effect.

Stillness Draw is a defining part of Ronin's identity. Its advantage is damage and impact, not Wraith-level reach.

It does not provide invulnerability, automatic parry behavior, or guaranteed armor during preparation.

## Dash Attack — Breaching Slash

Attacking during the approved window after the universal neutral dash produces **Breaching Slash**.

Breaching Slash is Ronin's quick and convenient sword option.

Working properties:

- fast startup after the dash,
- medium sword reach,
- moderate additional forward movement,
- moderate health damage,
- moderate enemy-posture damage,
- narrow-to-medium frontal coverage,
- lower stagger than Ronin's basic heavy attacks,
- and quicker recovery than Severing Cut, Crushing Cross, or Bloodfall.

Primary uses:

- punish a small opening that is too short for Severing Cut,
- reach a ranged or retreating enemy,
- attack after avoiding a dangerous move,
- finish a weakened enemy,
- and give Ronin one responsive attack without changing its overall heavy cadence.

Breaching Slash deliberately deals less damage, posture pressure, and stagger than Severing Cut. It does not automatically enter Crushing Cross or Bloodfall.

The player returns to Ronin's normal options after the attack.

## Parry Counter — Answering Steel

After a successful universal parry, pressing Attack produces **Answering Steel**.

Answering Steel is a fast precise retaliatory cut with:

- universal parry requirements and timing,
- little forward movement,
- narrow coverage,
- high health damage,
- extremely strong enemy-posture damage,
- and controlled recovery into defense.

Ronin does not receive a larger parry window, easier timing, automatic parries, or extra invulnerability.

The counter is stronger because the player earned the opening through the same universal parry used by every Aspect.

## Defensive profile

Strong blocking and parry payoff support Ronin's grounded heavy style.

Ronin's working defensive direction is:

- high player-posture capacity,
- more efficient sustained blocking than Wolf or Wraith,
- universal parry timing,
- average-to-slow natural posture recovery,
- no posture recovery while actively guarding,
- and no instant defensive cancel from a committed heavy strike.

This allows Ronin to remain stable in direct exchanges long enough to identify a punish opportunity.

The defensive advantages are balanced by:

- slow attacks,
- minimal attack movement,
- low tracking,
- severe whiff recovery,
- and slower posture recovery after pressure accumulates.

Ronin does not gain immunity to posture break, automatic counters, free damage while blocking, protection from grabs, or safety from perilous attacks and hazards.

## Attack cancellation and commitment

Ronin's damage requires meaningful commitment.

Ronin cannot:

- begin Bloodfall and immediately cancel into block,
- prepare Stillness Draw and freely parry at the final moment,
- erase a missed heavy attack with an instant dash,
- or rotate a committed strike dramatically toward a moving enemy.

Defensive access returns after the attack's meaningful recovery rather than during the important startup and active frames.

Breaching Slash may recover more quickly because its damage and stagger are intentionally lower.

## Damage, posture, and stagger profile

| Property | Working direction |
|---|---|
| Normal range | Conventional medium sword range |
| Sequence length | Three attacks |
| Cadence | Slowest and most deliberate |
| Per-hit health damage | Highest of the initial three |
| Sustained output | Lower and more opening-dependent than Wolf |
| Enemy-posture pressure | Highest or near-highest per clean strike |
| Ordinary-enemy stagger | Strongest of the initial three |
| Attack movement | Minimal and grounded |
| Tracking | Low-to-moderate, decreasing with commitment |
| Coverage | Strong frontal arcs without Wraith's extended reach |
| Whiff risk | Severe on Crushing Cross, Bloodfall, and Stillness Draw |

Against ordinary enemies, clean Ronin hits may interrupt light attacks, create stronger recoil, or stagger weaker targets according to enemy rules.

Elites and bosses retain their normal armor and interruption rules. Ronin expresses power against them through large health and posture chunks rather than constant interruption.

## Natural strengths

- highest per-hit health damage,
- highest or near-highest per-hit enemy-posture pressure,
- strong ordinary-enemy stagger,
- excellent punishment of short and large openings,
- powerful elite and boss damage,
- defining Held Attack damage,
- strong Parry Counter payoff,
- and stable blocking during direct exchanges.

## Natural weaknesses

- slow attack startup,
- severe whiff recovery,
- low sustained output when openings are scarce,
- limited tracking,
- minimal attack-bound movement,
- mobile enemies,
- ranged pressure,
- attacks from several directions,
- and accumulated player posture that recovers slowly.

## Encounter viability

### Mixed groups

Ronin uses Severing Cut for a priority target, Crushing Cross for enemies grouped in front, blocking and parrying to stabilize an exchange, and Breaching Slash when a quick response is required.

### Crowds

Ronin can heavily stagger or eliminate individual ordinary enemies and threaten grouped frontal targets. It struggles when fast enemies attack from multiple directions before a heavy strike can complete.

### Ranged enemies

Universal dash, Breaching Slash, projectile defense where supported, prosthetics, and target prioritization provide access. Ronin does not receive Wolf's pursuit or Wraith's extended reach.

### Elites and bosses

Ronin excels at converting readable openings into large health and posture progress.

- Small opening: Severing Cut or Breaching Slash.
- Moderate opening: Severing Cut → Crushing Cross.
- Large opening: full basic sequence or Stillness Draw.
- Successful parry: Answering Steel.

The player judges how much commitment the opening safely permits without managing a separate combo system.

### Hazards

Minimal attack movement reduces accidental travel into hazards, but area denial can remove the stable space Ronin needs to prepare heavy attacks.

## Distinction from Wolf and Wraith

- Wolf attacks fastest, moves forward through attacks, and wins through sustained four-hit pressure.
- Wraith attacks from the longest range with short two-hit poke sequences and spectral line or arc control.
- Ronin attacks slowest from medium range and wins through raw per-hit damage, posture impact, stagger, and strong punish options.

Ronin must not become a slower Wraith. Stillness Draw is defined by power, while Pale Lance is defined primarily by reach.

Ronin must not become a stronger Wolf. Wolf owns sustained output, pursuit, tracking, and pressure frequency; Ronin owns individual impact.

## Technique build space

Universal Techniques may naturally:

- **reinforce:** heavy Basic Attacks, Stillness Draw, stagger, direct damage, and enemy-posture chunks,
- **broaden:** attack speed, pursuit, crowd handling, ranged access, or sequence flexibility,
- **compensate:** whiff recovery, tracking, posture recovery, mobile enemies, or surrounding pressure,
- **hybridize:** Breaching Slash, Answering Steel, deathblows, blocking, posture, or prosthetics.

Ronin does not own all damage, posture, parry, block, Held Attack, or heavy-attack Techniques.

## Blood-katana presentation

Ronin's Returning Blood should make the katana feel:

- dense,
- compressed,
- heavy at impact,
- disciplined rather than wild,
- and capable of briefly accumulating power around the blade and sheath.

Possible presentation includes:

- a dense Blood edge,
- a spectral sheath during Stillness Draw,
- restrained trails before contact,
- large impact emphasis at the hit frame,
- and heavy audio rather than permanently enlarged reach.

Exact animation, VFX, color, shape, and audio remain open.
