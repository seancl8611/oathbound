# Blood Aspect System

Blood Aspects are controlled specializations of Akio's Returning Blood. They are not independent spirits or unrelated magic schools; each is a behavioral expression of the same altered Blood within him.

## Current Aspect families

### Wolf

A direct, predatory identity focused on pursuit, pressure, marked prey, blood trails, aggression, and finishing wounded targets.

### Wraith

An evasive and spectral identity focused on mist, afterimages, displacement, reduced presence, and attacks that feel partially detached from Akio's physical position.

### Ronin

A disciplined martial identity focused on parries, Counter Cuts, blade control, timing, and refined punishment rather than overt bestial transformation.

## Tier structure

Use the standard labels:

- Tier 0 — no active mutation
- Tier I
- Tier II
- Tier III
- Tier IV

Advancing a Tier should increase both mechanical benefit and visible consequence. Final bonuses, drawbacks, thresholds, and reset rules remain subject to balancing.

## Visual production rule

Do not create twelve full replacement Akio animation libraries. The preferred approach is:

- One approved base Akio animation set
- Modular eyes, veins, markings, weapon treatments, auras, mist, trails, and limited silhouette accents
- Aspect-specific VFX layered over the base animation
- Tier escalation through composable overlays

## Design constraints

- Aspects must alter tactics without replacing the katana game.
- Tier changes must be readable without overwhelming combat telegraphs.
- Drawbacks should create decisions rather than make a Tier objectively unusable.
- Aspect-specific effects should inherit the shared Returning Blood visual language.
