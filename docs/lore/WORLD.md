# World

Oathbound takes place primarily on an isolated Japanese-gothic island sealed behind an ancient barrier.

## The island

The island is not a dead ruin. Beast Blood preserves fragments of function:

- Soldiers continue patrols and defensive drills.
- Workers repeat their former labor.
- Animals retain hunting and pack behavior.
- Rituals continue after their meaning has been lost.
- Leaders protect places and people that can no longer be restored by preservation alone.

## The Strand

The Strand is the persistent hub from which Akio prepares, recovers, trains, and returns to the island. It is connected to the run loop through the Boat and houses the game's primary meta-progression characters and interactibles.

## Hushiro Gate Village

A wet, militarized village threshold. It remains recently human: timber, mud, soot-black tile, lanterns, barricades, guard buildings, cellars, and broken defensive infrastructure.

## Yomori Grove

A deeper region shaped by spirits, mist, lantern rites, roots, predators, and fungal or fire-rite pressure. Its exact history and local culture require further documentation.

## Kagutsuchi Court

The island's court and endgame region. It expresses formal power, ritual preservation, elite defenders, hollow vessels, and the final authority of the Shogun.

## The Wellspring

The supernatural source or concentration point associated with the island's preservation and the Shogun's oath. Exact cosmology and final interaction remain under review.
