# Story Overview

Oathbound takes place on an isolated island that once supported a prosperous kingdom led by the ruler who later became the Eclipse Shogun.

## Fall of the kingdom

The Shogun's civilization discovered an ancient source capable of producing Beast Blood. The source's exact nature and the manner of its discovery remain unresolved. Greed, worship, forbidden research, or attempts to exploit the power preceded the kingdom's fall.

The Shogun initially opposed the use of Beast Blood and sought to restrict access to it. His position changed when a deadly plague threatened the island and his daughter became one of the dying.

Beast Blood was first used as a cure. It appeared miraculous: the sick recovered, damaged bodies healed, and the kingdom survived. Use then expanded from the critically ill to soldiers and eventually to much of the population.

Corruption emerged over time. The Blood strengthened and regenerated its bearers while magnifying fear, ambition, loyalty, pride, hunger, and other impulses until restraint and humanity began to fail.

The Shogun remained coherent longer than most. He had been a popular ruler, a strong protector, and the center of a loyal civilization. Those same qualities gradually hardened into possessiveness, control, and an unwillingness to relinquish the power that had saved his people.

## The contained island

An ancient barrier now isolates the island, its corrupted inhabitants, and the source of Beast Blood. The barrier's builders, mechanism, and exact relationship to the source and Shogun remain unresolved.

Once every ten years, the Blood Moon empowers existing Beast Blood and weakens a narrow crossing path. The Order uses that opening to send one prepared warrior toward the island. Every attempt before Akio failed to produce a returning warrior.

The island's inhabitants are not mindless infected. Many retain fragments of memory, loyalty, recognition, training, attachment, and territorial identity. Corrupted soldiers and subjects may still follow the Shogun and defend their homeland, while other victims have become beasts, failed bodies, or spirit-like remnants.

## Akio's mission

Akio is a quiet, disciplined warrior of the Order sent to end the island's threat. The Order opposes Beast Blood and equips its crossing warriors with anti-corruption rites and warding magic.

Akio is exposed to Beast Blood but does not succumb normally. His resolve and the Order's warding contribute to an unprecedented condition called Returning Blood. It reconstructs him after death while preserving his identity and allows controlled Blood Aspects during runs. The exact origin remains under design lock.

Across repeated attempts, Akio travels through Hushiro Gate Village, Yomori Grove, and Kagutsuchi Court toward the Eclipse Shogun and the deeper source of Beast Blood.

## Successful-run direction

The Shogun remains the central character antagonist and principal ruler of the island, but he is not the original source of Beast Blood.

The current successful-run direction is:

1. Akio defeats the Shogun's current manifestation.
2. The victory opens temporary access to the ancient source or its protected inner chamber.
3. Akio damages or removes one persistent protective layer around the source.
4. The source, chamber, or completed act destroys or expels Akio's current body.
5. Returning Blood reforms him at the Strand.
6. The damage to the source persists across successful runs.

The exact number and nature of the layers, required successful clears, source encounter, forced-return event, and final ending are unresolved.

## Final-antagonist direction

The Eclipse Shogun remains responsible for choosing, expanding, defending, and potentially weaponizing Beast Blood. He should not become an innocent puppet whose actions no longer matter.

The ancient source is the deeper supernatural threat beyond him and is intended to support a true-final confrontation after the Shogun's permanent defeat. Its consciousness, motives, physical form, and combat identity remain open.

## Current themes

- Power discovered through greed and forbidden curiosity
- A miracle whose delayed cost corrupts the civilization it saved
- Loyalty becoming possessiveness
- Protection becoming control
- Human desire intensified by supernatural power
- Resolve delaying corruption without guaranteeing safety
- A quiet warrior using forbidden power without accepting its rule

## Canon boundaries

Exact dialogue, Akio's complete character arc, the source's nature and discovery, the Order's knowledge, the Shogun's present objective, successful-clear count, final source encounter, ending, and postgame explanation remain under development.
