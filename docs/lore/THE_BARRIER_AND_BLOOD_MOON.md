# The Barrier and Blood Moon

An ancient barrier contains the island and serves as the outside world's primary defense against Beast Blood.

## Barrier rules

- The barrier was created by powerful figures in the past.
- It is not a mechanism the Order can casually disable, alter, or bypass.
- It normally prevents reliable entry and survival.
- Its exact builders, mechanism, and relationship to the Shogun remain unresolved.

## Blood Moon cycle

Once every ten years, the Blood Moon naturally empowers Beast Blood and weakens the containment barrier.

This creates a narrow crossing window:

- the Order may send one exceptionally prepared warrior through the viable path,
- Beast Blood across the island becomes more active,
- the crossing is treated as a rare mission rather than routine travel,
- all previous attempts before Akio failed to produce a returning warrior.

The Order does not normally use Beast Blood to empower crossing warriors. Akio's Returning Blood is an exceptional event, not the standard entry method.

## Boat and crossing

The Boat carries Akio through the narrow path opened during the Blood Moon. Before Akio, every departure was considered one-way. His Returning Blood remains linked to the island's Wellspring and the Strand-side Bloodwell, allowing repeated crossings during the active Blood Moon period.

## Story function

The Blood Moon explains:

- why the Order cannot send an army,
- why attempts occur only once per decade,
- why Akio's mission happens during an unusually active corruption state,
- why his ability to return changes the practical meaning of the crossing.

## Open questions

- Who created the barrier?
- Does it weaken uniformly or only along specific paths?
- Can the Shogun influence its behavior?
- What exactly happens when the active Blood Moon period ends?
