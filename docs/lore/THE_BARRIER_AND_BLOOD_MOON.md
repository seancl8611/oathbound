# The Barrier and Blood Moon

An ancient barrier contains the island and serves as the outside world's primary defense against Beast Blood.

## Barrier rules

- The barrier was created by powerful figures in the past.
- It is not treated as a device the Order can casually disable or alter.
- It normally prevents reliable entry and survival.
- Its exact builders, mechanism, and relationship to the Shogun remain to be finalized.

## Blood Moon

Once every few years, the Blood Moon naturally empowers Beast Blood and weakens the barrier.

This creates a rare infiltration window:

- The barrier is weak enough for an exceptional trained warrior to cross.
- Beast Blood throughout the island becomes more active.
- Any Beast Blood within an infiltrator would also be empowered, but the Order does not routinely rely on this as an entry method.

## Story function

The Blood Moon explains why the Order cannot repeatedly send ordinary forces, why Akio's mission occurs at a rare moment, and why the island's corruption is especially active during the game.

## Open questions

- Exact recurrence interval
- Whether the barrier weakens uniformly or at specific crossings
- Whether the Shogun can influence it
- What happens when the Blood Moon ends during an active run
