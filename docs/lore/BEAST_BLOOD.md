# Beast Blood

Beast Blood is the corrupting supernatural force contained on the island.

## Established properties

- It empowers bodies while eroding identity and judgment.
- It preserves instincts, roles, habits, and oaths after the person carrying them has begun to disappear.
- It can produce humanoid corruption, animal mutation, spirit-like manifestations, and hostless masses.
- Its expression differs according to the host, environment, history, and repeated behavior it inhabits.
- It becomes naturally empowered during the Blood Moon.

## Narrative function

Beast Blood is not random visual corruption. It externalizes what a person, creature, or institution refuses to release. A soldier remains a soldier, a protector remains a protector, and a hunter remains a hunter, but the role becomes detached from humane purpose.

## Relationship to Returning Blood

Returning Blood is not a separate original substance. It is Beast Blood changed by submitting to Akio's resolve.

See [Returning Blood](RETURNING_BLOOD.md).

## Visual discipline

Beast Blood generally uses restrained dark crimson, blackened veins, wet coagulated materials, mist, fissures, and host-specific distortions. It should not default to bright neon-red magic.
