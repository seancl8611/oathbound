# Beast Blood

Beast Blood is a rare supernatural power drawn from an ancient source associated uniquely with the island. Its exact source, form, location, and history of discovery remain unresolved during story design lock.

## Established properties

- Beast Blood can heal disease and injury, strengthen the body, extend life, and support unnatural regeneration.
- Its benefits carry progressive corruption, physical mutation, impaired judgment, and eventual loss of restraint.
- It intensifies qualities and motivations already present in the bearer rather than assigning every victim one repeated role or behavior.
- Fear may become violent self-preservation; loyalty may become possessiveness; pride may become domination; hunger may become predation.
- Strong resolve can delay or control transformation, but resolve may weaken under pain, fear, age, desperation, or approaching death.
- A bearer who remains composed may later surrender to the Blood deliberately or lose control when pushed near death.
- It becomes naturally more powerful during the Blood Moon.

## Acquisition and containment boundary

Beast Blood is not an airborne plague, a bite-transmitted infection, or a common substance that spreads through ordinary proximity.

Access requires deliberate use of Blood obtained from the island's ancient source or another specifically established source-derived process. The exact method by which usable Blood is drawn, stored, administered, and replenished remains open until the ancient source is designed.

This rarity is essential. The danger is not a conventional outbreak; it is the possibility that people will exploit, transport, weaponize, or distribute a forbidden power.

## Retained humanity and coexistence

Corruption does not immediately erase memory, recognition, loyalty, training, or attachment.

The island's inhabitants may still recognize former comrades, rulers, handlers, families, territories, and customs. These fragments help corrupted soldiers, villagers, animals, and other inhabitants coexist without requiring a hive mind or a universal Beast Blood hierarchy.

They may attack Akio because he is an armed outsider, a warrior of the Order, and an intruder entering defended territory. Akio carrying Returning Blood does not automatically make him one of them.

## Manifestation range

Beast Blood may produce:

- partially human corrupted people,
- full or partial beast transformations,
- altered animals,
- unstable masses or failed bodies,
- spirit-like remnants and wraith manifestations,
- environment-bound corruption.

These outcomes do not require a rigid taxonomy. Host, duration, will, physical condition, environment, and manner of use may all influence the result. The exact relationship between long-term Beast Blood exposure and Area 2's spirit forms remains under development.

## Ancient source boundary

The source is an ancient living or once-living supernatural power capable of granting the power of the beast. A giant Heart, core, vessel, remnant, organism, god, parasite, or another form remains possible, but none is currently canon.

The following remain unresolved:

- what the source truly is,
- whether it is alive, dormant, dying, or repeatedly awakening,
- whether it possesses consciousness, only primal impulse, or no personal intention,
- where usable Beast Blood physically comes from,
- and how the Shogun's civilization first found and accessed it.

Its discovery route must be designed after its nature is established. The civilization may have uncovered it beneath the island, encountered it after a supernatural event, brought it from elsewhere, or stored a source recovered through an earlier expedition; no option is locked.

## Relationship to Returning Blood

Returning Blood is not a separate original substance. It is an unprecedented condition created when Beast Blood fails to consume Akio's identity and instead reconstructs him while preserving continuity of self.

Akio's resolve and the Order's anti-corruption warding are current required ingredients. Their exact interaction and the reason Akio succeeds where previous warriors failed remain open in [Returning Blood](RETURNING_BLOOD.md).

## Visual discipline

Beast Blood generally uses restrained dark crimson, blackened veins, wet coagulated materials, fissures, host-specific distortions, and controlled organic pressure. It should not default to bright neon-red magic or conventional zombie-infection imagery.
