# Eclipse Shogun

The Eclipse Shogun predates the Order. He was once the island's protector and ruler.

## Fall

When his people faced destruction, he used Beast Blood to preserve them. The decision was not initially motivated by cruelty. It was an attempt to refuse catastrophic loss.

The preservation succeeded in the narrowest sense: bodies, duties, places, and patterns endured. Over time, protection became imprisonment. The Shogun continued enforcing an oath whose human purpose had already collapsed.

## Final-boss identity

The Shogun should remain recognizable as a protector even at his most monstrous. His authority, martial discipline, and refusal to release the island are central to the encounter.

The final conflict contrasts two relationships with Beast Blood:

- The Shogun uses it to prevent loss through permanent control.
- Akio forces it to obey while continuing to accept death, return, and change.

## Production implications

- He must not visually resemble a member or founder of the Order.
- Boss phases should preserve identity while revealing increasing oath-bound corruption.
- Wellspring, oath, barrier, and island-preservation imagery should converge in his presentation.

## Open questions

- His personal name and historical title
- The exact catastrophe he attempted to prevent
- Whether he created, inherited, or became bound to the barrier
- The final choice or ending after his defeat
