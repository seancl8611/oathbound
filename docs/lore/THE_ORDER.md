# The Order

The Order is the religious-military organization that trains and sends Akio toward the island.

## Established direction

- The Order exists after the fall of the Shogun's kingdom and does not share his institutional origin.
- It recognizes Beast Blood as a rare, corrupting power that must remain contained.
- It opposes the deliberate use, transport, and exploitation of Beast Blood.
- It prepares one exceptional warrior for the once-per-decade Blood Moon crossing opportunity.
- All prior crossing attempts failed to produce a returning warrior.
- It does not normally use Beast Blood to empower its warriors.
- Akio's Returning Blood is an exceptional event, not standard doctrine.

## Warding tradition

The Order has developed anti-corruption rites, seals, protective markings, and warding magic for warriors expected to confront Beast Blood.

These protections are intended to preserve judgment, memory, and spiritual integrity long enough to complete the mission. They do not grant immunity and have not previously allowed a warrior to return after death.

The current Returning Blood direction assumes that Akio's warding and resolve both contribute to his unprecedented survival. The exact rite and mechanism remain under design lock.

## Role in the story

The Order provides Akio's martial discipline, ritual language, equipment tradition, beast-hunting tools, warding methods, and mission.

Its complete historical knowledge remains unresolved. The Order may understand the barrier and danger of Beast Blood without knowing the ancient source's true nature, the Shogun's original plague-era decision, or the consequences of destroying the source.

Its intended response to Akio's transformation also remains open. Returning Blood may make him the Order's greatest opportunity and a doctrinal threat at the same time.

## Visual identity

Order equipment should feel functional, ritualized, and controlled:

- wrapped limbs and layered working garments,
- anti-corruption seals and containment marks,
- practical sword fittings,
- tool straps, pouches, cords, and prosthetic storage,
- field gear built for dangerous crossings rather than ceremony,
- restraint rather than ornate heroism.

## Open questions

- What does the Order believe the island's ancient source to be?
- How much does it know about the plague and the Shogun's original decision?
- What specific outcome does it expect from killing the Shogun or destroying the source?
- Was Akio expected to return under any circumstance?
- How does the Order react after learning he carries Returning Blood?
- How does it communicate with the Strand during the active Blood Moon period?
