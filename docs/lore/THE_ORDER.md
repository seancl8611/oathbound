# The Order

The Order is the religious-military organization that trains Akio, maintains the island containment operation, organizes the Strand, and sends him toward the island.

## Origin and historical duty

The Order began after the fall of the island's kingdom.

Survivors, mainland warriors, ritualists, and religious authorities could not destroy the corrupted kingdom or reach whatever sustained it. They created the barrier as a desperate containment solution and accepted responsibility for guarding it until the curse could be destroyed from within.

The Strand was established at the barrier's controlled threshold. The Keeper, a former noble of the island, aided the founders and bound his spirit to that passage.

The earliest members possessed more direct knowledge of the catastrophe than the modern Order. Over generations, much of that history was lost, fragmented, or reduced to inherited doctrine and ritual procedure.

The modern Order retains the essential duty:

- maintain the barrier and Strand operation,
- watch for the Blood Moon,
- train and select exceptional warriors from its ordinary ranks,
- prepare a warrior for the crossing,
- and destroy the cursed force and whatever sustains it.

Its warriors are not recruited through prophecy or known supernatural destiny. Akio joins as a skilled swordsman through ordinary service and is selected because he is capable of attempting the mission.

## Established knowledge

- The Order knows Beast Blood can both empower and corrupt island bearers.
- Earlier Blood Moon expeditions failed to return.
- The modern Order lacks full knowledge of the Heart, the Shogun's plans and the extraction history.
- Its anti-corruption tradition has yielded one **experimental physician-made protective binding** intended to preserve humanity, not immortality.
- Akio is selected for skill, begins uninfected, and becomes the device's first field-test wearer.

## Mission

The Order gives Akio a direct extermination mission:

1. Cross the barrier during the Blood Moon.
2. Destroy the island's cursed forces and any ruler or leader sustaining them.
3. Find and destroy the source that allows the curse and its apparently deathless bearers to persist.

The Order assumes that destroying the source will end the threat. It does not possess enough knowledge to warn Akio about the Heart's true nature or the consequences of destroying it.

The mission is considered one-way. Akio is expected to complete as much of it as possible, not return with a report.

The player's opening begins at the Strand as Akio prepares to cross for the first time, receives the physician's experimental protective binding, and departs to the ordinary Hushiro route.

## Warding tradition

Generations of seals, ritual practice and medicine have aimed to protect memory, judgment and spiritual integrity. They provide no general immunity.

A Strand physician develops a novel experimental binding for Akio. During an early direct Beast Blood exposure it does not exclude the curse but begins adapting to it. Its participation in preserving human identity through unexpected reconstruction was neither planned nor proven reproducible.

The device does not summon Akio to the Strand or select his destination.

## Role in the story

The Order trains Akio, gives the mission, maintains the barrier and Strand, prepares the Boat, and supplies the physician's device. The physician becomes a major Strand researcher associated with Bloodwell upgrades, while the Scribe retains the archival and human perspective.

The Shogun has no role in Akio's initial infection. The Heart and the island's history are uncovered through exploration.

## Visual identity

Order equipment should feel functional, ritualized, and controlled:

- wrapped limbs and layered working garments,
- anti-corruption seals and containment marks,
- practical sword fittings,
- tool straps, pouches, cords, and prosthetic storage,
- field gear built for dangerous crossings rather than ceremony,
- restraint rather than ornate heroism.

## Deliberately unresolved

- the exact founders and ritual used to create the original barrier,
- what fragmentary records first led the Order to begin sending warriors,
- whether it knows the Eclipse Shogun's name or only that the cursed forces have a ruler,
- how much communication remains possible between the Strand and the wider Order,
- and how the wider Order reacts after Akio unexpectedly returns carrying Returning Blood.
