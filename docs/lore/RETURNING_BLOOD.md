# Returning Blood

Returning Blood is Beast Blood that has been forced into obedience by Akio's resolve.

## Origin

Akio was not born immune, naturally compatible, or routinely prepared with Beast Blood by the Order. When the Blood claimed him, his refusal to surrender identity or purpose forced it to submit rather than erase him. That submission changed the Blood within him.

## Established properties

- It reconstructs or restores Akio after death, returning him fully human in outward identity rather than as a mindless corrupted host.
- It retains the power and adaptability of Beast Blood while responding to Akio's will.
- It can specialize into controlled Blood Aspects.
- It remains dangerous. Obedience is not the same as harmlessness.
- Repeated Embrace decisions can make its effects more visible and mechanically consequential.

## Gameplay connections

Returning Blood supports:

- Roguelite revival and return to the Strand
- Blood Aspects
- Corruption accumulation
- Resist/Embrace Shrine decisions
- Bloodwell progression
- Blood Mirror trials or reflection

## Canon restrictions

Do not describe Akio as immune to Beast Blood.

Do not describe Returning Blood as a standard Order technique.

Do not imply that all Order warriors are dosed with Beast Blood to cross the barrier unless this canon is explicitly changed later.
