# Oathbound enemy combat — current behavior vs proposed target

**Baseline:** `agent/akio-checkpoint-b-playtest`, commit `c756090c42831512d750f5dfbad4c928e6143d7d` (Nov 5, 2026). **Scope:** Engineering design review for Hushiro Corrupted Swordsman, then extension to other enemies. **Status:** This inventory records the original baseline and target decisions. **The first Hades-inspired Swordsman policy is now implemented experimentally** in the working branch; consult [HADES_STYLE_SWORDSMAN_V1_PLAYTEST.md](HADES_STYLE_SWORDSMAN_V1_PLAYTEST.md) for current implemented-versus-pending details. Nothing here is artist-commissioned or merged to main.

## Core objective

Make the Swordsman individually legible, deliberate and punishable while multiple enemies interact coherently. Let Hushiro's **authored waves** and mixed archetypes create difficulty; avoid continuous independent scoring of elaborate microbehaviors. Keep current good work: hits, guard/parry, action commitment, impact-pressure coordination, source-based directional rendering.

**Telemetry baseline (Nov 5 Akio-enemy-proxy playtest):** two Swordsmen often stayed less than 35 world units apart (about 45% of joint samples); across 260 tactical decisions they changed intent 96 times; brief sharp circles and animation snaps were observed. These observations are baseline symptoms, **not** acceptance tests of a new system. Full sprite-transition telemetry was not captured, so do not attribute every flicker to the AI without another test.

## One-owner architecture rules

1. **EncounterSpawner owns when/where new enemies spawn.** Tactical coordinator does not spawn enemies or change authored waves.
2. **Hushiro melee positioning coordinator owns the desired engagement destination/role.** A Swordsman may request position but cannot independently run an alternate orbit planner.
3. **The Swordsman's finite state/controller owns the current high-level behavior and requests action starts.** No concurrent tactical scorers disagree.
4. **PressureDirectorV2 owns admission of dangerous impact windows.** A position lease does not grant an attack or reserve a hit window.
5. **CombatActionRunner owns action phase/commitment.** Existing legacy coroutines remain the execution backend until replaced and verified.
6. **EnemyMotor owns motion composition.** Separation Runtime is a collision-safety last resort, not an independent chase/retreat planner.
7. **DirectionalActorPresentation owns visuals only** and reads reliable gameplay state. Its fallback to Idle must be explicit and traceable.
8. **All special effects, stun, death, target loss and room unload** override the normal plan with clean release of leases/reservations.

## Initial design acceptance criteria (PROPOSED; tune after baselines)

- One Swordsman: purposeful approach; readable warning, committed hit/whiff, recovery; no forced circular pacing.
- Two Swordsmen: stable complementary destinations; neither continuously re-enters the other's body clearance, without making combat one-at-a-time.
- Start from existing 45% of paired samples at <35 units; seek materially lower overlap (<15% is a **candidate** goal, not a universal hard-coded threshold).
- Avoid frequent engage/strafe/re-engage flips; measure **intent reversals per 10s**, slot reassignments, total distance travelled in circles, pair corrections per 10s.
- Preserve realistic missed lunges when Akio dodges; require root-telegraph/impact geometry evidence before tuning tracking.
- Guard, hurt and death visual fallback may remain temporary but must be detectable, not mislabeled a renderer overload.
- Preserve original hitbox/HP/posture/guard/prosthetic/pressure contracts; no actor duplication or alternative source folder.
- Verify one Swordsman, two Swordsmen, and two Swordsmen plus Archer, including respawn/room exit and the next authored wave.
