# Oathbound Game Overview

Oathbound is a 2D top-down action roguelite built around disciplined sword combat, posture pressure, precise parries, deathblows, and run-based progression.

The player controls Akio, a warrior of the Order sent through an ancient barrier onto a cursed Japanese-gothic island. The island is shaped by Beast Blood, a supernatural force that preserves roles, instincts, and duties after identity has begun to collapse.

Akio is not immune to Beast Blood. His resolve forced it to obey rather than erase him, mutating it into Returning Blood. This gives him the ability to return after death and develop controlled Blood Aspects during runs.

## Core player fantasy

The player should feel like a disciplined warrior surviving a place where duty has outlived humanity. Victory comes from reading intent, controlling rhythm, and choosing how much power to accept from the corruption within Akio.

## Structural references

- Sekiro-style posture, parry, and deathblow logic
- Hades-style run structure and hub return
- Bloodborne-adjacent tragic horror and material tone

These are directional references, not templates to copy.

## Current high-level structure

- The Strand: persistent hub and meta-progression space
- Area 1: Hushiro Gate Village
- Area 2: Yomori Grove
- Area 3: Kagutsuchi Court
- Final confrontation: Eclipse Shogun and the Wellspring
