# Endgame, Postgame, and Release Scope

This file owns the approved launch-level endgame, canonical postgame, completion, and release-presentation package. It closes top-level game scope without adding another large progression or challenge layer.

# Canonical ending state

The first true-final Heart victory completes the main story.

Akio destroys the Heart's manifested combat body and permanently cripples the ancient source. The Heart survives only as a faint regenerating remnant.

The permanent result is:

- the Heart can never again produce or release **new Beast Blood**,
- Beast Blood can never be deliberately extracted from it again,
- no new person or creature can become a Beast Blood bearer,
- the curse cannot spread to the mainland or any new population,
- the Shogun's expansion plan is permanently defeated,
- the Heart's remaining activity is reduced to a local containment/suppression problem.

Existing Beast Blood does not change. Akio, the Eclipse Shogun, corrupted inhabitants, altered beasts, and other existing bearers retain the Blood already within them and its established benefits, including regeneration/reconstruction where applicable.

Akio therefore retains Returning Blood, Blood Aspects, supernatural regeneration, and the ability to reconstruct after death. The Shogun can also reconstruct again after the canonical ending.

The player should leave the ending understanding that the world-threatening spread has been permanently stopped even though the island and its existing cursed population continue to exist.

# Canonical postgame premise

The completed save continues canonically rather than reverting to a pre-ending timeline.

The Heart slowly regenerates physical tissue because regeneration is fundamental to its nature. It can recover enough local mass to become dangerous and manifest another combat body, but it can never recover the lost ability to create or propagate Beast Blood.

Akio continues returning to the island to:

- monitor the remaining Beast Blood population,
- defeat continuing hostile forces,
- suppress the Shogun and other recurring threats,
- destroy regenerated Heart manifestations before their local activity becomes dangerous.

Postgame is therefore **continued containment**, not a second campaign and not a non-canonical reset.

# Postgame run selection

After Story Complete, the Boat presents two run goals before departure.

## Standard Expedition

**Hushiro → Keeper → Yomori → Twin Maws → Kagutsuchi → Shogun → results.**

The run ends after defeating the Shogun's current body.

This is the shorter postgame mastery/build route and uses the established three-region structure.

## Heart Suppression

**Hushiro → Keeper → Yomori → Twin Maws → Kagutsuchi → Shogun → Heart.**

The run continues directly into the regenerated Heart manifestation using the approved Shogun-to-Heart recovery handoff.

This is the full postgame mastery route and represents Akio suppressing the Heart's regrowth.

The route choice occurs before departure rather than after the Shogun so the player commits to the intended run length and objective in advance.

# Repeat Heart rewards

Postgame does **not** add a new Heart currency, Heart material, or permanent progression tree.

A Heart Suppression clear may provide:

- the normal persistent resources/mastery earned during the run,
- a modest completion bonus using existing Mist/Scroll reward language if tuning needs it,
- +1 Heart Suppression Clear record,
- clear time,
- final build record,
- selected Aspect,
- personal-best notification where applicable.

Exact bonus quantities remain reward tuning. The reward must use existing systems rather than creating a fourth persistent currency.

# Postgame records

The Discovery Board or another compact records surface tracks launch-relevant mastery statistics:

- total attempts,
- successful Standard Expedition clears,
- Heart Suppression clears,
- fastest Standard Expedition,
- fastest Heart Suppression,
- Heart clears with Wolf,
- Heart clears with Wraith,
- Heart clears with Ronin,
- regional boss defeat counts,
- miniboss defeat counts,
- deepest first-attempt progress,
- first canonical Heart victory,
- overall completion percentage.

Launch does not require a permanent database of every historical run. The current result screen may show the just-completed build while persistent records focus on meaningful totals and personal bests.

# Completion goals

The canonical ending does not require every permanent system to already be complete. Postgame remains useful for finishing the game's existing mastery content.

The launch **100% Completion** target requires:

- Story Complete / first canonical Heart victory,
- all **10 Akio Bloodwell nodes** purchased,
- all **8 Run Infrastructure nodes** purchased,
- all **9 Blood Mirror nodes** completed,
- all **8 Prosthetics** unlocked and all **19 Prosthetic upgrades** purchased,
- all **10 Relics** collected,
- all **20 Relic mastery milestones** completed,
- all **50 Techniques + 10 refinements** discovered/recorded at least once,
- all required launch Blood Cavern / Blood Mirror trials completed,
- required launch Discovery Board collection completed,
- at least one Heart victory with **Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin**.

100% does **not** require arbitrary currency hoarding, extreme speedrun records, no-hit clears, a first-attempt Shogun/Heart feat, or large repeated-clear counters.

# Achievements

Target approximately **30 launch achievements**, distributed broadly across:

- story/campaign progression,
- bosses and Bindings,
- permanent progression/collection,
- build and Technique milestones,
- Relic/Prosthetic mastery,
- Blood Aspect milestones,
- trials,
- Heart victory and three-Aspect Heart mastery,
- 100% Completion.

Achievements should reward meaningful play rather than require extreme grind or one-in-a-million first-attempt performance.

Exact names and individual triggers are a later implementation/content pass within this approved volume.

# Launch challenge-system boundary

Oathbound does **not** require a Hades-style Heat/Pact system at initial release.

Also excluded from required launch scope:

- New Game+,
- escalating contract/modifier levels,
- endless mode,
- daily challenge runs,
- postgame enemy-variant package,
- postgame room-variant package,
- alternate postgame boss forms,
- a fourth Blood Aspect,
- another campaign,
- another permanent progression tree,
- another persistent currency.

These remain post-launch possibilities only if playable testing or player demand demonstrates a concrete need.

# Difficulty direction

Launch is balanced around **one intended core combat difficulty**.

Accessibility options improve usability, readability, and input comfort without requiring separate Easy/Normal/Hard encounter versions during initial production.

If broader difficulty modes or modifier systems are added later, they should be designed from actual playtest/player data rather than guessed before encounter tuning is complete.

# Front-end and save scope

Launch front-end requires:

- Continue,
- New Game,
- **3 save slots**,
- Settings,
- Credits,
- Quit where platform appropriate.

Save cards should display at minimum:

- playtime,
- current Binding/story state,
- Story Complete marker when earned,
- completion percentage after that system becomes relevant.

Deletion requires explicit confirmation.

Autosave should protect permanent rewards and major state changes. Save-safe points include permanent reward acquisition, Strand state changes, boss/campaign milestones, and ending/postgame transitions.

A long 45–60 minute run should support quit-and-resume from safe room/checkpoint boundaries rather than requiring uninterrupted play. Exact checkpoint implementation remains technical design.

# Settings and accessibility

Required launch settings/accessibility scope includes:

## Controls

- full keyboard/mouse rebinding,
- controller rebinding,
- controller glyph support,
- vibration on/off,
- vibration strength where practical,
- hold/toggle alternatives where an action supports them.

## Visual/readability

- screen-shake control,
- reduced flashing option,
- reduced intense-VFX option where needed,
- UI scale,
- text-size options,
- high-contrast interaction/readability support,
- gameplay-critical information not communicated by color alone,
- damage-number toggle if damage numbers ship.

## Text

- dialogue text-speed control,
- instant-text option,
- manual advance,
- readable dialogue/background treatment,
- localization-safe text containers.

## Audio

Separate controls for:

- Master,
- Music,
- SFX,
- Ambience.

Full spoken dialogue VO is not part of launch scope, so a dedicated dialogue-voice mixer is not required unless voice scope changes later.

# Localization

**English is the required launch language.**

All player-facing text systems must nevertheless be localization-ready from production start, including:

- dialogue,
- Techniques,
- Relics,
- Prosthetics,
- menus,
- achievements,
- Discovery Board/codex,
- settings,
- tutorial/help text.

Additional launch languages may be promoted later according to budget/market/platform requirements, but the base production plan does not assume a large multi-language translation package.

# Credits and legal

Launch credits must cover applicable contributors and licensed dependencies, including:

- development,
- art/animation contractors,
- music,
- sound,
- writing/editing,
- QA,
- localization where applicable,
- middleware/tools requiring attribution,
- licensed assets/fonts where applicable,
- special thanks.

Required third-party license notices and platform/store legal requirements are included where applicable.

# Story-complete communication

After the first Heart victory, the save receives a permanent **Story Complete** state.

The ending and first postgame Strand state must directly communicate:

- the Heart was not completely erased,
- its pulse/regrowth is now faint,
- it can never create or spread Beast Blood again,
- existing bearers remain unchanged,
- Akio's continued mission is suppression/containment.

A concise core message is:

> **The Heart survived. Its curse cannot spread.**

Postgame NPC dialogue may acknowledge containment and repeated suppression, but launch does not require a new full narrative arc after credits.

# Scope closure

With this package approved, Oathbound's **top-level launch architecture is closed at paper-design depth**.

The next production-detail passes should focus on content realization and playable validation rather than new systems:

1. author regional standard-encounter pools and encounter compositions,
2. design/tune regional miniboss and boss encounters,
3. design/tune the two-form Heart encounter,
4. realize exact narrative scripts/content,
5. implement and playtest numerical balance, timing, rewards, and clear-time targets,
6. final release polish/QA/localization according to this scope.
