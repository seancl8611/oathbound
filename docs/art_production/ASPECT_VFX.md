# Blood Aspect VFX

Blood Aspect presentation communicates each kit's weapon geometry, commitment, Blood state, and impact before spectacle. Exact timings/counts follow implementation and playtesting.

## Shared rules

- The physical katana remains visible or clearly connected to the Blood-formed expression.
- Direction, reach, collision language, and Akio's final position must remain readable.
- VFX cannot imply tracking, homing, teleportation, invulnerability, armor, or target correction that gameplay does not provide.
- Tier escalation extends the Aspect's visual family rather than replacing it.
- Reuse base animation/effect families when a Tier changes values/geometry rather than creating a new action.
- Current combat does not assume universal player Posture, Deathblow/execution, or one shared parry/counter mechanic across all Aspects.

## Wolf — pressure and pursuit

Wolf uses dense close-range Blood edges, compact predatory trails, strong connected-contact emphasis, and clearly player-directed pursuit movement. Blood Hunt should read as one committed chosen line with a strong endpoint, not an autonomous wolf companion or homing cinematic.

Fanged Guard, where the current implementation retains it, must read as a narrow authored frontal protection window attached to the qualifying Wolf commitment rather than general armor or a Posture resource.

## Wraith — reach and spacing

Wraith uses elongated spectral geometry tied to the katana, restrained body effects, and clear dissipation at the end of authored reach. Long attacks should read as chosen lines/corridors rather than detached homing projectiles.

Any older presentation requirement that depended specifically on a universal Deathblow/execution or universal parry event is superseded by the current Combat V2 contract and must be re-authored only if the current Wraith kit still needs an equivalent mechanic.

## Ronin — impact and stability

Ronin uses dense compressed Blood edges, restrained buildup, strong hit-frame/recoil emphasis, minimal noise between major impacts, and the clearest Guard/Reprisal language in the roster.

Ronin's current repeated defensive growth is **Health-chip Guard efficiency**, not maximum player Posture. Do not create a Posture-capacity HUD/VFX family for it.

Falling Mountain/Deep Rupture should read as heavy point impact and delayed ground-bound force. Measured Weight/Perfect Weight and Shattering Wake should emphasize deliberate heavy pressure, guard recoil, and impact without implying hidden tracking or automatic target correction.

## Production boundary

Aspect VFX are presentation clients of current gameplay. When an older Aspect document or runtime seam still contains retired Posture/Deathblow assumptions, reconcile that mechanic before commissioning final VFX for it rather than treating the stale reference as art scope.
