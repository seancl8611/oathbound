## Planned scope

- Keeper, Peddler, Smith, Raven, Undead Samurai, and Scribe
- Strand docks, paths, stations, and environmental kit
- Boat
- Forge
- Discovery Board, including scalable Technique records
- Bloodwell and reformation presentation
- Blood Cavern outer training hall
- Blood Mirror inner chamber
- Merchant, Forge, Discovery, Bloodwell, Mirror/trial, run-results, and pause/overview UI
- Trial-state, temporary-loadout preview, completion, reward, Technique-pool unlock, and mastery presentation

The full in-run Technique reward, replacement, reserve, and refinement interface remains Milestone 4 scope. Milestone 3 only needs the trial and codex states required by the Strand.

## Dependency rules

- NPC service and progression behavior must be documented before final interface art.
- Blood Mirror UI inherits the Blood Aspect, Technique trial, and reward boundaries established in gameplay documentation.
- Trial screens require fixed Aspect, Technique, prosthetic, or Relic loadout, completion, reward, and mastery data before final layout.
- Technique trial loadouts must read as temporary and clear when the trial ends.
- Bloodwell return effects follow finalized persistence and successful-return rules.
- Physical stations and primary landmarks are approved before final hub layout and lighting.
- Hub navigation must be tested through repeated return loops, not only as a single establishing scene.
- Individual NPC and station files own detailed requirements; this milestone owns their production grouping.

## Completion test

All six NPCs and major services can be located, distinguished, interacted with, and understood without visual confusion. Training, Aspect trials, Technique demonstrations, permanent progression, revival, results, and repeated departure all work as one coherent hub loop.
