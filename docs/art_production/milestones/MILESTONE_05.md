## Planned scope

- Lingering Wraith
- Lantern Wraith
- Mist Shepherd
- Stalker Hound
- Two Area 2 minibosses
- Rootfang and Briarthorn boss structure
- Spirit, mist, lantern, root, fungal, predator, and fire-rite effects
- Yomori environment kit, functional rooms, props, hazards, arenas, and UI extensions

## Suggested internal order

1. Region visual development and base environment kit
2. Standard enemies and dependent effects
3. Minibosses and arena additions
4. Rootfang
5. Briarthorn and paired encounter integration
6. Functional rooms and full regional pass

## Completion test

- Spirit targetability, flicker, and reappearance states remain readable.
- Lantern casts communicate through the lantern focal point.
- Mist Shepherd buff ownership is understandable through audio and restrained links.
- Stalker Hound pounce remains fair, infrequent, and clearly telegraphed.
- Enemy roles are distinct and environmental ambiguity never becomes unfair.
- The paired boss communicates links, vulnerability, transfer, and defeat states.
- The region inherits project-wide scale and UI rules.
