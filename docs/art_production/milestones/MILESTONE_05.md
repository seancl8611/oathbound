# Milestone 5 — Complete Area 2

## Goal

Produce Yomori Grove as a complete regional combat, environment, VFX, room, miniboss, and boss package.

## Planned scope

- Lingering Wraith
- Lantern Wraith
- Mist Shepherd
- Stalker Hound
- Two Area 2 minibosses
- Rootfang and Briarthorn boss structure
- Spirit, mist, lantern, root, fungal, predator, and fire-rite effects
- Yomori environment kit, functional rooms, props, hazards, arenas, and UI extensions

## Suggested internal order

1. Region visual development and base environment kit
2. Standard enemies and dependent effects
3. Minibosses and arena additions
4. Rootfang
5. Briarthorn and paired encounter integration
6. Functional rooms and full regional pass

## Completion test

Spirit and mist mechanics remain readable; enemy roles are distinct; environmental ambiguity never becomes unfair; the paired boss communicates links, vulnerability, transfer, and defeat states; and the region inherits project-wide scale and UI rules.
