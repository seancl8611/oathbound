# Milestone 6 — Area 3 and Endgame

## Goal

Complete Kagutsuchi Court, its combat roster, major encounters, Eclipse Shogun, Wellspring, and ending-space art.

## Planned scope

- Court Guard
- Court Caster
- Elite Defender
- Hollow Vessel
- Court Sentinel
- Blood Lotus encounter
- Eternal Swordsman
- Eclipse Shogun and all phases
- Court projectiles, ritual zones, revive/vessel states, Lotus effects, and final-boss effects
- Kagutsuchi environment kit, rooms, arenas, Wellspring, transitions, and regional props
- Endgame boss UI and completion presentation

## Dependency rules

- Final enemy and boss mechanics must be locked before specialized VFX production.
- Eclipse Shogun concept, phases, arena, Wellspring, and ending presentation form one dependency chain.
- The final boss should receive dedicated internal batches rather than one undivided delivery.
- Final visuals must preserve the Shogun's identity as a protector predating the Order.

## Completion test

Elite and revive mechanics remain readable at high encounter density; Court effects preserve safe-space information; the Shogun's phases communicate distinct response rules; and the final sequence unifies the island, oath, barrier, Beast Blood, and Wellspring visual language.
