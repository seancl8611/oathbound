## Planned scope

- Court Guard
- Court Caster
- Elite Defender
- Hollow Vessel and Spillborn support
- Court Sentinel
- Blood Lotus Heart, Stalks, fissures, punishment projectiles, and multi-cycle presentation
- Eternal Swordsman
- Eclipse Shogun and all three revelation phases
- Court projectiles, ritual zones, revive states, spawning, shield coverage, frenzy, Lotus effects, and final-boss effects
- Kagutsuchi environment kit, rooms, Blood Lotus arena, duel court, Wellspring throne-space, transitions, and regional props
- Endgame boss UI and completion presentation

## Suggested internal order

1. Remaining standard enemies and dependent effects
2. Blood Lotus Heart, Stalks, arena fissures, and multi-cycle UI
3. Eternal Swordsman duel package
4. Eclipse Shogun concept and Phase 1
5. Phase 2, Wellspring arena control, and first arena transformation
6. Phase 3 sovereign-beast form and full eclipse state
7. Final defeat, ending transition, and complete endgame integration

## Dependency rules

- Court Guard and Court Caster revival requires explicit first-death, interruption, completion, and final-death states.
- Elite Defender revival remains unresolved and must not be added unless shield identity stays clear.
- Hollow Vessel spawn ownership and Spillborn contrast must be approved before mixed-encounter integration.
- Court Sentinel frenzy needs a distinct transition and recovery state.
- Blood Lotus requires synchronized Heart, Stalk, fissure, punishment, posture, and UI state design.
- Eternal Swordsman animation quality and duel readability take priority over spectacle.
- Eclipse Shogun concept, phases, polearm continuity, Wellspring arena, and ending presentation form one dependency chain.
- The final boss receives dedicated internal batches rather than one undivided delivery.
- Final visuals preserve the Shogun’s identity as a protector and sovereign predating the Order.

## Completion test

- All five standard-enemy roles remain distinct at high encounter density.
- Revive, guard, spawn, and frenzy states are understood immediately.
- Blood Lotus communicates active Stalks, punishment, core exposure, posture, deathblow readiness, and reset cycles.
- Eternal Swordsman remains a clean, respectful duel.
- Eclipse Shogun’s three phases reveal deeper truth while preserving one coherent sovereign identity.
- Court effects preserve safe-space information and the final sequence unifies the island, oath, barrier, Beast Blood, eclipse, and Wellspring visual language.
