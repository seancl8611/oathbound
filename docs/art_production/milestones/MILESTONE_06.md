## Planned scope

- Court Guard
- Court Caster
- Elite Defender
- Hollow Vessel and Spillborn support
- Court Sentinel
- Blood Lotus Heart, Stalks, fissures, punishment projectiles, and multi-cycle presentation
- Eternal Swordsman
- Eclipse Shogun and all three approved revelation phases
- Court projectiles, ritual zones, revive states, spawning, shield coverage, frenzy, Lotus effects, and Shogun effects
- Kagutsuchi environment kit, rooms, Blood Lotus arena, duel court, Shogun throne-space, transitions, and regional props
- Ancient Heart chamber with one historical broken outer Binding and six intact campaign Bindings
- Shogun-built extraction apparatus positioned against the exposed Heart
- One reusable successful-run ritual: blood offering, Heart reaction, one Binding rupture, Akio dissolution, and Strand reconstruction transition
- Six removable or destroyable Binding-layer states plus the fully exposed Heart state
- Progressive Heart exposure and stronger room-local pulse, movement, sound, and reaction
- Binding campaign-progress UI showing six player clears
- True-final Phase 1 concept and production: the freed mobile Heart with malformed limbs
- True-final Phase 2 concept and production: an enormous nonhuman vessel formed around the Heart
- One transformation between the two Heart phases
- Heart-chamber arena support for both forms
- Two-phase true-final boss UI
- First-clear ending and credits presentation
- Shortened repeat-clear Heart presentation
- Postgame route access presentation after system design is locked

The ancient Binding identity, Court-built extraction apparatus, Returning Blood rejection, one-Binding-per-run rule, forced dissolution, persistent damage rule, seven-original/six-remaining count, six successful Binding clears, seventh final-story run, two-phase Heart concept, canonical ending, and repeatable non-progressing Heart route are locked.

This does **not** require a different apparatus, puzzle, Binding mechanism, or fully unique win animation for every successful clear. The base ritual should be reusable, with escalation delivered through removable layers, additional exposed Heart art, stronger room-local reactions, altered effects, and limited state variants.

The base game also does **not** require island-wide visual transformations, universal enemy modifiers, rebuilt regional environment sets, new enemy families, or entirely different Shogun fights after every successful clear.

The true-final Heart does **not** require a humanoid swordsman form, a third phase, or a separate weak-point or body-part targeting subsystem.

Exact Heart attacks, movement timings, posture behavior, arena rules, animation counts, VFX counts, tuning, recovery between bosses, and postgame route controls remain later encounter-design and playtest dependencies. They should not be invented or quoted as locked production before those mechanics are approved.

Future difficulty settings, run modifiers, enemy or room variants, challenge restrictions, alternate controls or conditions, and special Heart-route rewards remain deferred expansion ideas and are not mandatory Milestone 6 scope.

Kagutsuchi Court remains the approved Area 3 setting. The milestone does not assume the discarded Eclipse Palace, underground-city, living-citadel, or layered-palace alternatives.

## Dependency rules

- Court Guard and Court Caster revival requires explicit first-death, interruption, completion, and final-death states.
- Elite Defender revival remains unresolved and must not be added unless shield identity stays clear.
- Hollow Vessel spawn ownership and Spillborn contrast must be approved before mixed-encounter integration.
- Court Sentinel frenzy needs a distinct transition and recovery state.
- Blood Lotus requires synchronized Heart, Stalk, fissure, punishment, posture, and UI state design.
- Eternal Swordsman animation quality and duel readability take priority over spectacle.
- Eclipse Shogun concept must be reviewed against the regal false-master direction before final character quotation; the approved high-level reference is Kokushibo-like composure, aristocratic menace, disciplined swordmaster presence, restrained speech, and controlled monstrous escalation without copying protected anatomy, costume, weapon, motifs, silhouette, or attacks.
- Defeating the Shogun, entering the Heart chamber, using the extraction apparatus, breaking one Binding, dissolving Akio, and returning to the Strand form one reusable dependency chain for the first six successful clears.
- The seventh successful story run defeats the Shogun's current body and continues directly into the two-phase Heart encounter while the run remains active.
- Destroying the Heart makes Beast Blood inert, stops the Shogun's reconstruction, ends the curse, and makes Akio mortal.
- The Heart Bindings are ancient and mysterious; the extraction apparatus is a later Court construction. Art must keep those visual languages distinguishable.
- Ordinary extraction offers fresh human blood inward and collects Beast Blood outward. The successful-run ritual reuses that apparatus with Akio's resistant Returning Blood.
- No physical channel network is required to continuously empower or control the island's bearers.
- Heart art may imply a divine organ, island core, vessel, or remnant, but must not conclusively explain its cosmic origin or default to a simple realistic oversized heart.
- The Unbound Heart and Vessel of Continuance must remain large nonhuman creature forms and must not become another swordsman encounter.
- The encounter must use established combat systems and must not add a weak-point subsystem solely for this boss.
- The Shogun and Heart receive dedicated internal batches rather than one undivided delivery.

## Completion test

- All five standard-enemy roles remain distinct at high encounter density.
- Revive, guard, spawn, and frenzy states are understood immediately.
- Blood Lotus communicates active Stalks, punishment, core exposure, posture, deathblow readiness, and reset cycles.
- Eternal Swordsman remains a clean, respectful duel.
- The Eclipse Shogun remains the central character antagonist and culminates in a deliberate Beast Blood transformation representing false ascendancy rather than true control.
- Defeating the Shogun clearly opens a new Heart Binding step during the first six successful clears rather than appearing to end the campaign immediately.
- The successful-run ritual reads clearly without requiring a separate completion system for every Binding.
- Binding destruction, six-clear progress, and forced return read as permanent campaign progress.
- The seventh story run reads distinctly because the same active run continues from the Shogun to the Heart.
- The true-final Heart has exactly two readable large-creature phases and remains mechanically distinct without introducing an unsupported weak-point system.
- The first Heart victory clearly ends the curse and story.
- Repeat Heart victories clearly read as postgame challenge completions without additional story progression.
- Court and Heart effects preserve safe-space information throughout the endgame.
