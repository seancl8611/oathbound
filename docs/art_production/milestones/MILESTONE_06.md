# Milestone 6 — Area 3 and Endgame

## Goal

Complete Kagutsuchi Court, its combat roster, major encounters, Eclipse Shogun, Heart chamber, reusable Heart Binding completion sequence, true-final Heart confrontation, ending presentation, and repeat-clear presentation.

## Planned scope

### Kagutsuchi Court

- Court Guard
- Court Caster
- Elite Defender
- Hollow Vessel and Spillborn support
- Court Sentinel
- Blood Lotus
- Eternal Swordsman
- Eclipse Shogun
- Kagutsuchi environment kit and functional rooms
- Blood Lotus arena
- Eternal Swordsman duel space
- Shogun royal arena
- regional projectiles, zones, revive states, spawning, shield coverage, frenzy, and encounter UI

The Elite Defender remains a pure shield-and-spear positional defender and does not use the Court Guard and Court Caster revival mechanic.

### Eclipse Shogun

The current production boundary includes:

- one principal Area 3 boss,
- a regal and composed starting identity,
- controlled Beast Blood escalation,
- at least one materially inhuman later state,
- continued intelligence and recognizable character identity,
- and a clear handoff to the Heart chamber.

The earlier three-phase structure and phase labels remain draft encounter concepts. Exact phase count, weapon, attacks, transformation anatomy, sprite sets, animations, VFX, and arena states require a dedicated concept and gameplay-design lock before final quotation.

### Heart Binding campaign

- ancient Heart chamber,
- one historical broken outer Binding,
- six intact campaign Bindings,
- Court-built extraction apparatus,
- one reusable successful-run ritual,
- six removable or destroyed Binding states,
- fully exposed Heart state,
- progressive room-local Heart reactions,
- Binding campaign-progress UI,
- Akio dissolution and Strand reconstruction presentation.

The reusable ritual is:

1. Returning Blood offering,
2. Heart reaction,
3. one Binding rupture,
4. Akio dissolution,
5. Strand reconstruction.

The milestone does not require a separate apparatus, puzzle, mechanism, or unique completion sequence for every Binding.

### True-final Heart and ending

- Unbound Heart concept and production,
- Vessel of Continuance concept and production,
- one transformation between forms,
- arena support for both forms,
- two-form boss UI,
- Heart destruction and curse-ending presentation,
- first-clear ending and credits,
- shortened repeat-clear presentation,
- postgame route-access presentation after its control method is approved.

The Heart has exactly two conceptual forms. It does not require a humanoid swordsman form, third phase, or separate weak-point subsystem.

Exact Heart attacks, movement, posture behavior, arena rules, animation counts, VFX counts, tuning, and recovery between the Shogun and Heart remain later encounter-design and playtest dependencies.

## Scope exclusions

The initial milestone does not require:

- island-wide visual transformations after each Binding,
- universal enemy modifiers,
- rebuilt regional environment sets,
- new enemy families after each clear,
- a fully different Shogun fight after every clear,
- postgame difficulty modes,
- challenge modifiers,
- enemy or room variants,
- or special Heart-route reward systems before those mechanics are approved.

## Suggested internal order

1. Standard enemies and shared Court effects
2. Blood Lotus encounter package
3. Eternal Swordsman package
4. Eclipse Shogun concept and encounter-design lock
5. Eclipse Shogun production after design approval
6. Heart chamber and extraction apparatus
7. Reusable Binding-completion sequence
8. Six Binding states and campaign-progress UI
9. True-final Heart encounter design
10. True-final Heart production
11. Ending and credits presentation
12. Repeat-clear and postgame route UI after system approval

## Dependency rules

- Court Guard and Court Caster revival require clear first-death, interruption, completion, and final-death states.
- Elite Defender production focuses on shield orientation, repositioning, and spear punish windows without revival.
- Hollow Vessel spawn ownership and Spillborn contrast must remain readable.
- Court Sentinel frenzy needs a clear transition and recovery state.
- Blood Lotus requires synchronized Heart, Stalk, fissure, punishment, posture, and UI states.
- Eternal Swordsman readability and animation quality take priority over spectacle.
- The Shogun must receive concept and encounter approval before final character quotation.
- The Heart Bindings and Court-built extraction apparatus require distinct visual languages.
- The Shogun and Heart receive separate internal batches.
- The true-final Heart uses established combat systems and does not add a one-off weak-point system.

## Completion test

Milestone 6 is complete when:

- all five standard-enemy roles remain distinct,
- revive, guard, spawn, and frenzy states are readable,
- Blood Lotus communicates its complete encounter-state loop,
- Eternal Swordsman remains a clean duel,
- the Shogun reads as the central character antagonist and deliberate false master,
- the first six Shogun victories clearly lead to Binding completion,
- the seventh story victory clearly continues into the Heart,
- Binding progress and forced return read as permanent campaign progress,
- the Heart has two readable nonhuman forms,
- the first Heart victory clearly ends the story,
- and repeat victories read as non-canonical postgame challenge completions.
