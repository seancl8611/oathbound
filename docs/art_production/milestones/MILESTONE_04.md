# Milestone 4 — Player Combat Depth

## Goal

Complete the visual identities and interface support for Akio's major build-shaping systems after the base character, combat VFX hierarchy, and shared Shrine foundation are stable.

## Planned scope

- Wolf Prey Mark, pressure states, and Tier escalation
- Wraith afterimage, perfect-dodge, Mist-Step, and Tier escalation
- Ronin parry, Counter Cut, Focus, and Tier escalation
- Modular Tier I–IV mutation overlays
- Five stance VFX families: Storm, Frost, Ember, Hex, Shadow
- Eight prosthetic VFX families
- Final Aspect, stance, and prosthetic icons
- Boon and relic card families
- Currency, Health, Spirit, breakable, treasure, and reward-object art
- Status markers and damage-number extensions required by the above systems

## Suggested internal order

1. Final Blood Aspect icons and three base VFX identities
2. Tier escalation and modular mutation overlays
3. Prosthetic tool VFX and icons
4. Stance VFX, icons, and status-language pass
5. Currency and pickup family
6. Boon, relic, breakable, and reward-object family
7. Full HUD, Shrine, and mixed-build readability integration

## Dependency rules

- Final overlays inherit approved Akio sheets.
- Aspect effects inherit shared Returning Blood and Shrine language from Milestone 2.
- Tool effects follow approved tool role, timing, footprint, and status behavior.
- Stance effects follow approved tactical identities while exact balance remains tunable.
- Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin must remain distinct rather than becoming color variants.
- Mist Raven and Wraith reposition effects require separate silhouette and timing language.
- Status markers and damage-number types must be consistent across stances, prosthetics, enemies, boons, and HUD.
- Item-family quantities, costs, and drop tables must be locked before a final fixed quote; reusable templates may be produced before final catalog size.

## Completion test

- Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin are immediately distinct.
- Tier escalation is visible, modular, and compatible with the base player sheet.
- All eight prosthetics communicate footprint, target, status, and active state.
- All five stances communicate tactical identity without obscuring sword arcs or enemy tells.
- Currency, pickup, boon, relic, and reward families connect world sprites to UI icons and cards.
- Build-related icons and statuses remain understandable at gameplay and menu scale.
