# Milestone 4 — Player Combat Depth

## Goal

Complete the visual identities and interface support for Akio's major build-shaping systems after the base character and shared Shrine foundation are stable.

## Planned scope

- Wolf VFX family and Tier escalation
- Wraith VFX family and Tier escalation
- Ronin VFX family and Tier escalation
- Modular Tier I–IV mutation overlays
- Five stance VFX families
- Eight prosthetic VFX families
- Final Aspect icons and selection presentation
- Stance and prosthetic icons
- Boon, relic, item, consumable, currency, material, and Boss Emblem art families

## Dependency rules

- Final overlays inherit approved Akio sheets.
- Aspect effects inherit shared Returning Blood and Shrine language from Milestone 2.
- Tool VFX follow confirmed tool mechanics and activation timing.
- Item-family quantities must be locked before quotation.

## Completion test

Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin are immediately distinct; Tier escalation is visible but modular; all player effects preserve core combat readability; and build-related icons remain understandable at gameplay and menu scales.
