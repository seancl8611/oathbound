# Art Direction

## Style

High-resolution pixel art with strong silhouette discipline, restrained palettes, and polished animation. Blasphemous is a detail-quality reference, not a perspective reference; Oathbound is top-down/high-angle 2D.

## Tone

- Dark Japanese gothic
- Disciplined martial horror
- Wet, grounded, functional materials
- Tragic rather than flamboyant
- Elegant danger rather than visual excess

## Color hierarchy

- Cool desaturated environments
- Lantern amber as isolated warmth
- Muted life-red for health and blood
- Restrained dark crimson for Beast Blood
- Host- or mechanic-specific accent colors only where they improve readability
- Avoid neon corruption and indiscriminate glow

## Character rules

- Silhouette first
- Weapon direction and role visible at gameplay scale
- Subtle, partial, or no outlines
- Grounded poses and weighted motion
- Corruption should preserve the host's former identity and function
- Boss hierarchy comes from pose, mass, materials, animation quality, and encounter framing, not ornament alone

## Environment rules

- Modular construction
- Clear navigation and combat footprints
- Functional room types readable from composition and focal props
- Atmospheric layers separated from base tiles
- Background detail must not erase characters, hazards, projectiles, or UI

## VFX hierarchy

Frequent effects stay restrained. Parry, posture break, deathblow, Shrine choice, and boss phase transitions receive stronger visual priority.
