# Asset Inventory

This is the high-level production inventory. Individual files and milestone briefs own detailed animation and state lists.

## Player

- Akio base character and combat animations
- Core combat VFX
- Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin VFX families
- Tier I–IV modular mutation overlays
- Five stance VFX families
- Eight prosthetic VFX families

## Characters and combatants

- Area 1: 6 standard enemies, 2 minibosses, 1 two-phase boss
- Area 2: 4 standard enemies, 2 minibosses, Rootfang/Briarthorn boss structure
- Area 3: 5 standard enemies, Blood Lotus, Eternal Swordsman, Eclipse Shogun
- Strand: 6 recurring NPCs

## Environments

- Hushiro Gate Village base kit and functional room skins
- Yomori Grove base kit, rooms, arenas, hazards, and props
- Kagutsuchi Court base kit, rooms, arenas, Wellspring, and props
- Strand hub, docks, stations, and Blood Cavern/Mirror

## UI/UX

- Combat HUD
- Corruption, Aspect, and Tier module
- Shrine interface
- Miniboss and boss UI
- Hub progression screens
- Pause, overview, results, settings, controls, save/loading, and front end

## Items and rewards

- Boons
- Relics
- Consumables
- Currencies and upgrade materials
- Boss Emblems
- Chests, pickups, and breakables

## Presentation and release

- Dialogue and narrative presentation
- Boss titles and completion treatment
- Opening/ending art if used
- Logo, app icon, achievements, store capsule, key art, and trailer cards

## Inventory rule

When a system, enemy, boss, room type, or item family is added or removed, update this inventory and its assigned milestone in the same change.
