# Combat Stance VFX

Stance effects modify Akio's swordplay without replacing the approved base moveset. Each stance needs a distinct tactical read, status language, and payoff cue while preserving the weapon arc, parry timing, enemy silhouette, and safe-space information.

## Storm Stance

**Tactical identity:** aggressive chain-lightning pressure and multi-target awareness.

**Visual language:** narrow high-contrast electric arcs. The first target receives a focused hit; thinner secondary arcs bridge deliberately to chained targets. Shock uses a compact upper-body or weapon-side marker.

**Readability:** first target, chained targets, and Shock application must be separable at a glance. Target limits or internal cooldowns should look intentional rather than random.

**Constraints:** avoid wide storm clouds, screen-filling flashes, or arc density that hides attack tells.

## Frost Stance

**Tactical identity:** slow, buildup, freeze, and deliberate punish windows.

**Visual language:** cold desaturated blade trails, crystalline stack accents on body contours, a clean locked freeze state, and fast brittle shatter feedback.

**Readability:** chilled, near-freeze, frozen, and shattered states need distinct progression. The frozen overlay must preserve the enemy's silhouette and attack orientation.

**Constraints:** favor value shift and hard-edged shape accents over snow clouds or glitter.

## Ember Stance

**Tactical identity:** sustained Burn pressure and small scorched control zones.

**Visual language:** compact blade heat, directional ember sparks, body-seam burn accents, and defined low ground scorch areas.

**Readability:** burned targets and active scorch zones must be obvious without flooding the scene with orange. Ground-zone edges must remain distinct from ordinary floor texture.

**Constraints:** keep flames compact, purposeful, and tied to attacks or triggers.

## Hex Stance

**Tactical identity:** curse buildup, delayed punishment, and oppressive debuff play.

**Visual language:** one memorable dark curse mark family using restrained violet-red or blood-black accents, with a stronger doom or detonation transition at meaningful thresholds.

**Readability:** cursed targets and near-threshold targets must remain visible in dark environments. Delayed payoff should read slightly heavier than a normal hit without becoming an explosion.

**Constraints:** avoid intricate glyph detail that disappears at gameplay scale.

## Shadow Stance

**Tactical identity:** mark-and-consume play built around evasive timing, punish windows, and exposed targets.

**Visual language:** thin body-shaped afterimages, clear enemy marks, and a crisp center-mass consume flash.

**Readability:** marked targets, consume events, and exposure or posture payoff must be obvious. Afterimages cannot blur Akio's final position or enemy body lines.

**Constraints:** use a few strong silhouettes rather than foggy smears.

## Shared production rules

- Status colors are supporting identifiers, not the only identifier; shape and placement must also differ.
- Stance effects inherit Akio's base sword trails and approved animation timing.
- Final mechanics, switching rules, costs, and upgrade paths remain owned by gameplay documentation.
- Effects are tested in mixed encounters and over all three regional palettes before final approval.
