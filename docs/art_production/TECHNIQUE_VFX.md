# Technique VFX

Techniques reshape current combat actions through repeatable family effects. VFX must preserve sword paths, enemy tells, safe space, target facing, and the accepted small gameplay scale.

The current catalog contains **40 Techniques + 6 refinements**. Technique ownership is additive and has no inventory slots/global cap.

## Current global action triggers

Global Action Techniques may use:

- Basic Attack;
- Held Attack;
- Dash / Dash Attack.

Kit-specific events may support explicitly restricted Techniques/hooks, but art must not imply that a universal parry/counter or Deathblow/execution event exists for every Aspect.

## Family language

### Echo
Delayed pale-silver additional sword slashes. Echoes follow authored attack geometry rather than replaying Akio's whole body animation.

### Rupture
Gold/cracked buildup culminating in a compact direct-damage impact with strong hidden Poise/guard pressure and bounded nearby force. Rupture is a Technique-family mark, **not** a shared enemy Posture meter.

### Seal
Violet marks progress toward temporary Bind. One/two/three-mark states must remain distinguishable without color alone.

### Rift
One ivory fracture becomes more unstable as it is intensified, then opens for direct Health damage. It should not read as a projectile or a stack counter floating independently from the target.

### Crimson
Crimson communicates direct Health damage, genuine rear-position payoff, and **Vulnerable**. Vulnerable must not imply slow, stun, root, forced facing, or fake backstab access.

## Reuse hierarchy

Prefer base sword trails/hit feedback, Aspect language, and small modular family overlays before commissioning a bespoke effect. Frequent Basic-trigger effects remain light; Held and rarer family payoffs may carry more weight; Dash effects stay attached to the real authored movement/contact path.

## Readability constraints

- No Technique effect may hide enemy windups, projectiles, hazards, safe zones, or Akio's final position.
- Multiple Techniques may react to the same action, so effects must layer cleanly.
- Family marks must remain recognizable on moving enemies.
- Crimson must not obscure target facing.
- Technique art may visualize hidden Poise/guard pressure through impact quality, but must not recreate a universal visible Posture bar or Deathblow cue.

## Refinements

A refinement should look like a modest improvement to its parent Technique, not a separate ability or second full visual identity.
