# Decision Log

## 2026-07-10 — Returning Blood origin

**Status:** locked

**Decision**

Akio's resolve forced Beast Blood to submit rather than erase him. That submission mutated it into Returning Blood.

**Replaces**

Any implication that Akio is naturally immune or that Returning Blood is an ordinary substance used by the Order.

**Affected documents**

- `docs/lore/RETURNING_BLOOD.md`
- `docs/characters/AKIO.md`
- `docs/gameplay/BLOOD_ASPECTS.md`
- `docs/gameplay/CORRUPTION_AND_SHRINES.md`

## 2026-07-10 — Order and Beast Blood

**Status:** approved

**Decision**

The Order does not normally empower its warriors with Beast Blood. Akio's condition is exceptional.

**Affected documents**

- `docs/lore/THE_ORDER.md`
- `docs/lore/THE_BARRIER_AND_BLOOD_MOON.md`

## 2026-07-10 — Eclipse Shogun predates the Order

**Status:** locked

**Decision**

The Eclipse Shogun was the island's protector before the Order existed. He used Beast Blood to preserve his people, and protection became imprisonment.

**Affected documents**

- `docs/lore/ECLIPSE_SHOGUN.md`
- `docs/lore/STORY_OVERVIEW.md`
