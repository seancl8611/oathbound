# Decision Log

This file is a concise index of major approved directions that materially changed Oathbound's scope or identity.

It does not repeat complete rules. Current authoritative files always override this summary. Superseded drafts and earlier wording are recoverable through Git history rather than retained here.

## 2026-07-10 — Seven production milestones

Oathbound uses seven dependency- and playtest-based top-level art milestones with separately quoted internal batches.

**Authority:** `docs/overview/PRODUCTION_ROADMAP.md`

## 2026-07-10 — Paid Style Test gate

Milestone 1 begins only after a separately paid Style Test locks practical sprite scale, palette, perspective, detail, outline, shadow, and Godot import targets.

**Authority:** `docs/art_production/milestones/MILESTONE_01.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Blood Aspects replace the stance system

Wolf, Wraith, and Ronin are the central run identities. The former Storm, Frost, Ember, Hex, and Shadow progression system is removed.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/BLOOD_ASPECTS.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Four active Techniques and one reserve

Runs use four active Technique slots, one inactive reserve, independently useful Techniques, and at most one slotless refinement per Technique.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/TECHNIQUES.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Previewed reward framework

Room function and primary payout are separate. Routes may preview reward categories, Techniques appear at selected opportunities, Shrines own Resist and Embrace, rest rooms own recovery and reserve swapping, and shops use Gold.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/ITEMS_AND_REWARDS.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Currency ownership

Mist, Scrolls, and Boss Emblems persist. Gold is run-only. `Mist Shards` is deprecated unless intentionally restored.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/ITEMS_AND_REWARDS.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Area 2 encounter structure

The Embered Pilgrim and Rotwood Host are Yomori's minibosses. Rootfang and Briarthorn form Twin Maws; both begin active and the first defeated empowers the survivor.

**Authority:** `docs/content/area_2/BOSS.md`

## 2026-07-11 — Area 3 encounter sequence

Blood Lotus and Eternal Swordsman are Kagutsuchi's two minibosses. Eclipse Shogun is the regional boss.

**Authority:** `docs/content/area_3/OVERVIEW.md`

## 2026-07-14 — The Heart and ancient Bindings

Beast Blood comes from the Heart, which was already buried beneath the island and imprisoned by seven ancient Heart Bindings created by an unknown power.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/BEAST_BLOOD.md`

## 2026-07-14 — Plague-era extraction

The Court breached the outermost Binding during the plague and built an extraction apparatus. Fresh human blood was offered inward and Beast Blood was released outward without death or human sacrifice.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/STORY_OVERVIEW.md`

## 2026-07-14 — Beast Blood transmission boundary

Beast Blood must be deliberately introduced. It does not spread through ordinary contact, wounds, bites, soil, roots, water, prey, vegetation, or death on the island.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/BEAST_BLOOD.md`

## 2026-07-14 — Returning Blood origin

Akio descends from the Shogun's escaped child. His first death during the Blood Moon awakens inherited Beast Blood and reconstructs his established human form at the Strand.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/RETURNING_BLOOD.md`

## 2026-07-14 — Akio's true control

Akio is the only known bearer who can preserve genuine agency, Resist escalation, vary Blood Aspect expression, return toward baseline, and oppose the Heart itself.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/RETURNING_BLOOD.md`

## 2026-07-15 — Shogun responsibility and forced salvation

The Shogun remains intelligent and responsible. He intends to end containment and expand through conquest and forced Beast Blood salvation.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/ECLIPSE_SHOGUN.md`

## 2026-07-15 — Regional curse progression

Hushiro represents rupture, Yomori represents adaptation, and Kagutsuchi represents false ascendancy. Yomori's ecological damage is caused by corrupted inhabitants and beasts rather than contagious environmental Blood.

**Authority:** regional overview files

## 2026-07-20 — Heart Binding campaign

The Court destroyed one of seven Bindings before the game. Akio destroys the remaining six across six successful runs using one reusable Returning Blood ritual.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/RUN_STRUCTURE.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Final story run and Heart ending

The seventh successful story run continues from the Shogun into the two-form Heart encounter. Destroying the Heart ends Beast Blood, stops the Shogun's reconstruction, completes the story, and makes Akio mortal.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/STORY_OVERVIEW.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Postgame continuity

Completed saves retain repeatable normal runs and optional Heart-route gameplay. Repeat Heart victories do not create new canonical endings or alter the completed story.

**Authority:** `docs/gameplay/RUN_STRUCTURE.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Eclipse Shogun design depth

The Shogun's high-level identity is resolved as a regal, composed, disciplined false master with deliberate intelligent inhuman escalation. Exact phase count, phase labels, weapon, attacks, transformation anatomy, animation list, and VFX remain later encounter-design work.

**Authority:** `docs/lore/ECLIPSE_SHOGUN.md` and `docs/content/area_3/BOSS.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Elite Defender role

The Elite Defender remains the pure shield-and-spear positional defender and does not use one-time revival.

**Authority:** `docs/content/area_3/enemies/ELITE_DEFENDER.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Milestone 1 Posture Break Cue

VFX-005 Posture Break Cue is included in Milestone 1 Batch 2 with the Corrupted Swordsman and Deathblow Cue.

**Authority:** `docs/art_production/milestones/MILESTONE_01.md`

## 2026-07-21 — Current question structure

The design tracker contains only unresolved decisions that affect current scope, content volume, production planning, or narrative presentation. Exact playtest tuning remains in the owning gameplay or encounter file.

**Authority:** `docs/_meta/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` and `docs/_meta/ASSISTANT_WORKFLOW.md`
