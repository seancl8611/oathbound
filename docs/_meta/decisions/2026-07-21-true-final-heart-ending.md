# True-Final Heart, Ending, and Repeatable Postgame Route

## Decision

Story-Spine Question 12 is resolved.

After Akio destroys all six remaining Heart Bindings, the next successful full run becomes the seventh and final story run. The player completes the normal island route, defeats the Eclipse Shogun's current body, and continues into the Heart encounter without ending or losing the run between those bosses.

The Shogun does not require a separate permanent-death mechanic. The Heart is the source sustaining Beast Blood as an active curse. Destroying it makes Beast Blood inert, stops the Shogun's reconstruction, releases him from the Blood's influence, and allows his unnaturally sustained body to die permanently.

## True-final encounter concept

The encounter has exactly two conceptual phases:

1. **The Unbound Heart** — the Heart tears free of the prison and extraction apparatus, grows malformed limbs, and becomes a mobile beastlike organ.
2. **The Vessel of Continuance** — the Heart forms an enormous nonhuman defensive beast body around itself while remaining visibly central to that form.

The encounter is a large creature fight rather than another swordsman duel. It must not create a humanoid Heart warrior.

The encounter uses Oathbound's established combat systems. It does not add a separate weak-point or body-part targeting subsystem.

Exact attacks, movement patterns, timings, posture behavior, phase-transition rules, arena behavior, numerical tuning, animation list, VFX list, recovery between bosses, and accessibility adjustments remain future encounter-design and playtest work.

Returning Blood's established rejection of the Heart may support the final narrative and visual resolution without automatically creating a new player mechanic.

## Canonical first victory

The first Heart victory is the canonical end of the story.

Destroying the Heart:

- makes active Beast Blood inert,
- stops the Shogun's reconstruction and causes his permanent death,
- releases corrupted inhabitants before their unnaturally sustained bodies fail,
- collapses corrupted beasts and failed bodies,
- allows Yomori's lingering spirits to pass on,
- ends the supernatural Blood Moon cycle,
- and allows the containment barrier to weaken safely.

Akio survives in his current complete human body. Returning Blood, Blood Aspects, supernatural regeneration, and future reconstruction end with the source. Akio becomes mortal.

The ending and credits follow.

## Repeatable postgame route

After the canonical ending, the completed save remains playable.

Normal roguelite runs and the Heart route may be repeated. Repeat Heart victories are gameplay challenges only. They do not:

- advance the story,
- create additional canonical endings,
- imply that another Heart exists,
- reverse the completed ending,
- or change the island's canonical fate.

The postgame should eventually provide a clear choice or condition determining whether a run ends after the Shogun or continues into the harder Heart encounter. The exact Boat control, modifier, menu label, condition, reward structure, and presentation remain future design work.

## Deferred expansion ideas

Future postgame development may explore:

- additional difficulty settings,
- optional run modifiers,
- enemy variants,
- room variants,
- challenge restrictions,
- alternate controls or run conditions,
- special rewards,
- and mastery records.

These ideas are documented for later exploration and are explicitly outside the initial game scope.

## Initial production boundary

The initial scope includes:

- one Unbound Heart form,
- one Vessel of Continuance form,
- one transition between them,
- one Heart-chamber arena supporting both forms,
- two-phase boss presentation,
- first-clear curse-ending and credits presentation,
- shortened repeat-clear presentation,
- and a postgame route-access state once its control is designed.

The initial scope excludes:

- a third Heart phase,
- a humanoid Heart swordsman,
- a separate weak-point subsystem,
- a locked attack list before encounter design,
- additional canonical story clears,
- and the deferred postgame modifiers and variants.

## Superseded assumptions

This decision supersedes earlier language implying that:

- the Shogun must be permanently killed before Akio reaches the Heart,
- the Heart is a one-time-only boss that cannot be repeated,
- continued play must return the save to a strictly pre-ending story state,
- or Question 12 remains unresolved.
