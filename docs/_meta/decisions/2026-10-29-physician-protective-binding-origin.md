# 2026-10-29 — Physician protective binding and first-run origin

This decision supersedes the **Akio-specific** Shogun-caused infection, mandatory opening Shogun defeat and experiment origin recorded on 2026-10-03.

## Locked decisions

1. Akio begins completely human and uninfected.
2. An Order physician equips him with a first-field-test anti-corruption protective binding, intended to preserve identity, **not** immortality.
3. Start with a short Strand preparation, then normal Hushiro **Floor 1** gameplay.
4. An early authored **direct blood-bearing wound** introduces Beast Blood despite incomplete protection. Ordinary proximity/scratches/air do not routinely infect.
5. The binding and curse begin adapting together. At his first **genuine death** Beast Blood unexpectedly reconstructs Akio while the altered binding helps preserve recognizable humanity: Returning Blood.
6. Beast Blood powers regeneration and mutation; device regulation is partial, and Akio's rare individual response and developing discipline matter. No reliable replication formula.
7. The physician is the seventh major Strand NPC and oversees the **existing Bloodwell** progression fiction; the Scribe retains records, discoveries and Akio's human connection.
8. The Shogun does **not** originate Akio's curse or first resurrection. He is fascinated on later encounter.
9. Akio's subconscious survival instinct chooses the Strand spot. **No magical device tether or Strand anchor** is introduced.
10. A pre-awakening mastery run cannot destroy a Binding. Six post-awakening Binding clears, final Heart, ending, postgame and existing mechanics stay unchanged.

## Open design questions

Exact device design, physical fusion, physician ethics, first infection opponent, removal/failure, and supernatural identity-preservation mechanism. A two-part return tether or inevitable physician betrayal is not established.

## Implementation boundary

Narrative authority is updated here. Existing Godot runtime scripts and smoke tests may still reflect the old opening and require a separate implementation pass.
