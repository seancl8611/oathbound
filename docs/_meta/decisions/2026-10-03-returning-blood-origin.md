# 2026-10-03 — Returning Blood origin and opening narrative revision

This decision supersedes the earlier escaped-bloodline / dormant-inherited-Beast-Blood origin and the earlier fully unscripted first-death model.

## Locked origin

Akio begins the expedition as an ordinary human Order warrior with **no Beast Blood**.

The game begins late in Akio's first expedition. He has already reached the Strand, met its recurring NPCs, crossed onto the island, and progressed far enough to reach the Eclipse Shogun through his own skill.

The first Shogun encounter is playable and should respect player performance, but the narrative outcome is Akio's defeat.

After mortally wounding Akio, the Shogun becomes interested in the human who reached him without Beast Blood. His approved intent/working line is:

> "Let's see what the Blood makes of you."

Through his centuries-long bond with the Heart, the Shogun calls and directs a **large, source-near concentration of ordinary Beast Blood** from the island/Heart itself and forces it into Akio's mortal wound.

There is no separate refined "Shogun Blood." The material is the same Beast Blood; the exceptional circumstance is its concentration and proximity to the true source.

The Shogun does not intentionally stabilize, weaken, or engineer Akio's transformation. He expects death or corruption and does not know what the result will be.

Akio dies carrying that Blood. Instead of remaining dead or becoming a conventional beast, the Blood reconstructs him while preserving the continuity of his identity. This unprecedented relationship is **Returning Blood**.

## Why Akio remains deliberately unresolved

Beast Blood is a supernatural curse rather than a rigid biological system.

It affects people differently: some die, some transform, some retain more of themselves, and some become unusually powerful or specialized forms.

The Blood appears to work with what it finds in the bearer. Identity, intentions, emotion, resolve, circumstance, physical condition, duration, and manner of exposure may influence the result.

Resolve can support control, but willpower alone does not explain Akio. Akio and the Shogun are both exceptionally resolute; their different identities and intentions help shape different relationships with the same curse.

Akio's exact unprecedented compatibility is intentionally not reduced to genes, receptors, immunity, ancestry, or one hidden mechanism.

## Shogun experimentation

The Shogun has deliberately exposed selected people to Beast Blood before Akio to see what the curse would make of them.

Captives, enemies, followers, retainers, or promising warriors may have become past experiments. Some specific powerful creatures, minibosses, or bosses may use this origin where it strengthens their individual lore.

This is **not** the origin of every beast on the island.

This history explains why the Shogun knows he can direct Beast Blood into another person while preserving uncertainty about the outcome.

## Strand reconstruction

The Strand itself is not a magical resurrection anchor.

Returning Blood increasingly responds to Akio's conscious intent while he is alive. When Akio dies, conscious control disappears and the Blood falls back on deeper survival instinct.

Akio's strongest instinct is to return somewhere safe. The Strand is the safest place he knows during the expedition, so the first reconstruction occurs at one specific safe location there.

That successful return reinforces the same location as Akio's subconscious destination for later deaths.

The Keeper, Boat, Bloodwell, Order warding, oaths, seals, and Strand structures do not create this destination.

## Narrative delivery

Because Akio already used the Strand before the player first sees him return there, recurring NPCs already know him.

The first player-facing hub dialogue should react to his impossible return rather than introduce the characters.

The Scribe becomes the main recurring observer of Returning Blood. She can gradually establish that Beast Blood affects people differently, appears influenced by the bearer, rebuilds Akio, and increasingly responds to his intention/instinct without ever producing a definitive scientific explanation.

Useful approved dialogue intent includes:

> "The Blood does not make beasts from nothing. It works with what it finds."

> "I don't think the Blood is choosing the Strand, Akio. I think you are."

These are working intent lines rather than mandatory final script.

## Campaign continuity

This revision changes the opening and Returning Blood origin but does **not** reopen:

- the normal post-awakening 12 / 10 / 11 regional route,
- six remaining Heart Bindings,
- six successful Binding clears,
- the final Shogun-to-Heart story run,
- the ending where the Heart permanently loses the ability to create/spread new Beast Blood,
- existing bearers retaining their already-present Blood,
- canonical postgame containment.

## Superseded explanations

Retired from current canon:

- escaped island ancestor as Akio's power source,
- inherited/dormant Beast Blood in Akio before the expedition,
- royal-bloodline explanations,
- dormant Blood pulling Akio toward the island,
- first death occurring anywhere as the Returning Blood trigger,
- a mastery-player exception reaching the Heart before awakening,
- separate refined Shogun Blood,
- the Shogun deliberately stabilizing Akio's transformation,
- Order warding/oaths or the Strand itself anchoring resurrection.

Current lore authorities override older decision-history entries that preserve those superseded concepts as historical context.
