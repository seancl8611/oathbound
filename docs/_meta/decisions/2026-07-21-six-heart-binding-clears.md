# Six Heart Binding Clears and Final Run

## Status

Approved and locked at campaign-count, roguelite-progression, and production-boundary level.

## Decision

The Heart's ancient prison originally contained seven Heart Bindings.

Before the game, the Shogun's researchers destroyed the outermost Binding to expose the Heart and build the Beast Blood extraction apparatus. Six intact Bindings remain when Akio begins the campaign.

Akio must complete six successful Binding runs. Each one:

1. defeats the Eclipse Shogun,
2. reaches the Heart chamber,
3. uses the Court's extraction apparatus with Returning Blood,
4. destroys one remaining Binding,
5. dissolves Akio's current body through the Heart's retaliation,
6. and reconstructs him at the Strand with the destroyed Binding preserved.

Failed runs do not advance the Binding count.

After the sixth Binding is destroyed, the next successful full run becomes the seventh and final campaign run. No Binding remains, so Akio does not repeat the extraction ritual or suffer its automatic dissolution. He can make the Shogun's defeat permanent and continue into the true-final Heart encounter.

## Roguelite purpose

The six-clear requirement is a mastery objective rather than six unique story missions.

Repeated successful runs are supported by:

- alternate Blood Aspects,
- Technique builds and refinements,
- prosthetic choices and Prosthetic Techniques,
- Relics,
- route and reward variation,
- persistent progression,
- combat and boss mastery,
- and increasing player consistency.

The campaign should not minimize successful clears merely to reduce narrative repetition. Completing the full run several times is part of the intended roguelite experience.

## Required clear-to-clear presentation

The base game communicates progress primarily through the Heart chamber and existing progression presentation:

- one historical broken outer Binding,
- six removable or destroyed Binding states,
- progressively greater Heart exposure,
- stronger room-local pulse, movement, sound, and reaction,
- one fully exposed Heart state,
- a visible six-clear Binding tracker,
- and concise Shogun, NPC, results, or codex updates where appropriate.

The same extraction apparatus and core successful-run ritual are reused for all six Binding clears.

## Production boundary

The base game does not require the following after every Binding clear:

- island-wide visual transformations,
- universal enemy modifiers,
- rebuilt regional environment sets,
- new enemy families,
- unique extraction mechanisms,
- unique Binding puzzles,
- separate full cinematics,
- or entirely different Shogun encounters.

Those ideas may be considered later as optional expansion material after the base game is scoped and produced.

## Eclipse Shogun reference note

The approved high-level character reference for the Eclipse Shogun is Kokushibo from _Demon Slayer_. The intended qualities are:

- composed aristocratic menace,
- severe discipline,
- quiet confidence,
- restrained speech,
- overwhelming swordmaster presence,
- and controlled monstrous escalation that preserves intelligence and dignity.

This is not approval to copy the character. The Eclipse Shogun must have an original face, anatomy, silhouette, costume, armor, weapon, colors, motifs, attack effects, transformation, and movement language. Kokushibo's distinctive eyes, clothing, sword, moon motifs, effects, and exact mannerisms are not production requirements.

The Shogun's exact design, look, personality details, moveset, and transformation remain open for the dedicated Question 13 review.
