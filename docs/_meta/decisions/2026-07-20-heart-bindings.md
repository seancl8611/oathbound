# 2026-07-20 — Heart Bindings and Successful-Run Ritual

**Status:** locked at story-foundation and successful-run-loop level

## Decision

The Heart was already dormant and imprisoned beneath the island by multiple ancient **Heart Bindings** created by an unknown civilization or supernatural power.

The Shogun's civilization did not create the Bindings. During the plague, its researchers breached or destroyed the outermost Binding, stirred the Heart from deep dormancy, and built a separate extraction apparatus against the exposed living source.

Ordinary extraction uses a simple exchange:

- a small measure of freshly drawn human blood is offered inward through the apparatus,
- the Heart reacts,
- and Beast Blood is released outward into a collection vessel.

The offering does not require death or human sacrifice. The Court never fully understands why the Heart responds.

After each successful Shogun victory, Akio reuses the same extraction apparatus and offers Returning Blood. The Heart recognizes its own power and attempts to absorb and reclaim it, but Akio's Blood resists the Heart's control. The resulting rejection pulse breaks one remaining Heart Binding.

The Heart retaliates through the same contact and dissolves Akio's current body into blood. Returning Blood reconstructs him at the Strand using the stable human pattern and destination established by his first successful return.

The destroyed Binding remains gone because it is an external ancient restraint that the Heart did not create and cannot regenerate. Akio can destroy only one Binding per run because the retaliation kills his current body immediately after the rupture.

The successful-run presentation is deliberately simple and reusable:

1. Returning Blood offering,
2. Heart reaction,
3. one Binding rupture,
4. Akio's dissolution,
5. Strand reconstruction.

It does not require a separate mechanism, puzzle, apparatus, or unique win condition for every Binding. Later completions escalate through greater Heart exposure, stronger reactions, chamber deterioration, and limited state variants.

Destroying Bindings has a double consequence: it exposes the Heart's deeper vulnerable body while also weakening its imprisonment and strengthening its influence. Existing Beast Blood may react more intensely, and Akio's temptation toward the curse may increase, but no physical channel or continued Blood supply connects the Heart to every bearer.

## Strand-return boundary

The first complete Returning Blood reconstruction establishes the stable human pattern and Strand destination used by later returns. The deeper metaphysical transport remains deliberately mysterious.

The Bloodwell, Keeper, Boat, and Order warding do not create Returning Blood or power the revival.

## Replaces

- the earlier possibility that the extraction structure itself was ancient,
- generic undefined source-layer damage,
- any physical channel network continually feeding the Shogun or corrupted kingdom,
- and the possibility of a fully unique destruction mechanism for every successful clear.

## Still unresolved

- the total original and remaining Heart Binding count,
- the exact number of successful clears,
- the changes after each clear,
- the Shogun's reconstruction and permanent-defeat rule,
- the Heart's final vulnerability and true-final encounter,
- the ending and postgame explanation,
- and the deepest metaphysics behind the preserved Strand return imprint.
