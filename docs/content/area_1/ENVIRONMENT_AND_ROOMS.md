# Area 1 Environment and Rooms

## Base material language

- Wet timber walls and guard structures
- Packed earth, mud, wet stone, and damp wood floors
- Soot-black tile and low eaves
- Lit and unlit lanterns
- Gate fixtures, barricades, barrels, crates, debris, banners, broken weapons, and helmets
- Separate fog, runoff, incense, dust, and light-reference overlays

## Functional room skins

### Shrine

Weather-worn village shrine with a central basin, flame, seal, or equivalent focal object. Requires inactive, Corruption-ready, active, and used states.

### Rest

Sheltered guardhouse, boarded room, or hearth space. Warmest and softest Area 1 presentation, with no active threat language.

### Shop

Temporary salvage spread with a reserved merchant position, tagged goods, cloth, shelves, boxes, and a clear purchase focal area. The final merchant sprite belongs to the Strand/NPC milestone.

### Treasure/miniboss

Risk/reward space using a ruined training yard, barricaded courtyard, or mortuary route. Requires clear landmark dressing, reward object, and cleared state.

### Boss

Old gate threshold supporting Keeper of the Gate's full two-phase footprint and completion transition.

## Modularity rules

- Use the approved 32×32 base grid unless changed by the style test.
- Extend the Milestone 1 Hushiro kit rather than remaking it.
- Deliver modular pieces plus one assembled gameplay-scale example per room skin.
- Keep central combat footprints clear.
- Cap prop quantities and variants during quotation to prevent silent scope expansion.
