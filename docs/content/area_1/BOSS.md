# Keeper of the Gate

Keeper of the Gate is Hushiro's last true protector and the Area 1 major boss.

## Phase 1 — The Ashen Duelist

An almost-human master swordsman in higher-quality village armor. He defends Hushiro through disciplined spacing, posture pressure, varied rhythm, clean sword lines, iaijutsu distance closing, and distinct thrust/sweep branches.

Phase 1 should feel like the complete examination of Area 1 sword combat rather than an enlarged Corrupted Swordsman.

## Transition

The player's first deathblow cracks armor and restraint. Beast Blood pressure ruptures through the same recognizable warrior rather than replacing him with an unrelated creature.

## Phase 2 — The Collapse

The Keeper adopts a lower, wider, forward-pressured silhouette with torn cloth, blood-lit seams, unstable limbs, faster feral strings, an unblockable sweep, leap shockwave, and blood-driven lane charge.

## Identity rule

Face, armor remnants, blade, and core costume shapes should preserve continuity through the transformation.

## Arena

The old stone threshold where Hushiro ends and the inland road begins:

- Broken arch or gate
- Wet stone duel footprint
- Gate lanterns
- Defensive remnants
- Space for sweep radius, leap shockwave, and lane charge
- Phase-reactive atmosphere and a clear completion/exit state

## Readability requirements

- Overhead, thrust, and sweep branches must remain distinct.
- Phase 2 may be faster and more violent but not visually noisy.
- Sweep, shockwave, and charge must each communicate a different response rule.
- Boss UI, VFX, arena, and character states must remain legible together.
