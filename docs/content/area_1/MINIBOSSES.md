# Area 1 Minibosses

## Village Ogre

A massive human-derived garrison brute repurposed as defensive infrastructure. Mismatched military equipment and a slab-like fortification shield dominate his silhouette.

### Encounter purpose

- First heavy response-rule skill check
- Shield orientation and frontal coverage
- Slow commitment and large punish windows
- Parryable heavy attacks versus dodge-focused spin or space-clear attacks
- Tests whether the player reads startup, coverage, and recovery rather than attacking continuously

### Arena direction

Barricaded gate courtyard or ruined training yard with clear space for shield advances, overhead impacts, and circular threat radius.

## The Collector

Hushiro's former body gatherer, still dragging the dead through fog. A masked laborer with leather apron, wrapped forearms, and chains used as work tools turned weapons.

### Encounter purpose

- Awareness and rhythm pressure
- Long chain lash versus quick close punish
- Accelerating chain combo
- Snare/grab threat
- Controlled vanish and reappearance
- Dragging ground masses

### Arena direction

Mortuary street, corpse route, or narrow fog-heavy lane. Fog may reduce certainty but must never hide attack intent, Akio, HUD, or safe movement information.

## Hierarchy rule

Both minibosses must read above standard enemies through mass, animation quality, encounter framing, and mechanic complexity. Neither should visually compete with Keeper of the Gate's boss hierarchy.
