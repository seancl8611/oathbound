# Hushiro Gate Village

Hushiro Gate Village is Area 1 and the island's wet, militarized threshold. Discipline is still visible beneath corruption. The region should read as recently human first and monstrous second.

## Palette

- Rain-dark timber brown
- Soot-black roof tile
- Clay-mud earth
- Wet stone gray
- Smoke-gray fog
- Faded shrine red
- Dried blood brown-black
- Dim lantern amber
- Restrained dark-crimson corruption accents

The region remains desaturated overall. Warmth comes from lanterns; blood-red pressure should feel embedded beneath the scene rather than washing every surface.

## Material language

Dark soaked timber, warped clay walls, cracked tile, damp stone, soot-stained paper, rotting shrine wood, rusted iron, old cloth, household debris, and barricade material.

Corruption appears through staining, seepage, darkened grain, root intrusion, structural warping, and diluted Beast Blood emerging from cracks, cellars, and shrine-adjacent spaces.

## Enemy-family read

Enemies are recently human and partially transformed:

- villagers repeat fragments of final routines in twitchy groups,
- soldiers remain upright, role-bound, and disciplined,
- familiar human function remains visible beneath collapse.

The visual principle is human ruin first, full monstrosity second.

## Architecture

A dense dark-timber village of:

- narrow paths and compressed lanes,
- leaning homes and close rooflines,
- shallow balconies,
- worn torii and small shrines,
- guard points and gate infrastructure,
- barricaded interiors,
- cellars and work spaces,
- visible evidence of failed defense.

## Ambient pressure

- low drifting fog,
- lantern flicker,
- damp haze and roof runoff,
- cloth and paper movement,
- muddy ground,
- blood-slick thresholds,
- debris-choked alleys,
- quiet Beast Blood seepage.

## Movement philosophy

Villagers twitch, hesitate, rush, and repeat routes. Soldiers retain slower stance discipline, readable guards, and deliberate attack structure. Environmental movement remains subtle and oppressive through fog, runoff, lanterns, cloth, paper, and creaking wood.

## Combat purpose

Hushiro teaches and tests the complete core combat language:

- basic melee exchanges,
- fast animal pressure,
- weak swarm targets,
- ranged pressure,
- persistent hazards,
- restraint/controller threats,
- response-rule minibosses,
- fog and awareness pressure,
- a two-phase final combat examination.

## Roster

- Six standard enemies
- Village Ogre
- The Collector
- Keeper of the Gate

## Functional spaces

- Combat rooms
- Shrine
- Rest
- Shop
- Treasure/miniboss spaces
- Boss approach and old gate arena

## Production relationship

Milestone 1 establishes the base Hushiro combat kit and three enemies. Milestone 2 completes the remaining regional art, shared Shrine foundation, functional room skins, minibosses, and boss.
