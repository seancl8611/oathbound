# Hushiro Gate Village

Hushiro Gate Village is Area 1 and the island's wet militarized threshold.

## Regional identity

- Damp timber, packed earth, wet stone, soot-black tile, mud, fog, and low rooflines
- Lantern amber as isolated warmth against cool desaturated materials
- Garrison buildings, defensive gates, barricades, cellars, village homes, and ruined work spaces
- Recently human first, monster second
- Corruption embedded in familiar structures rather than total supernatural abstraction

## Combat purpose

Hushiro teaches and tests the complete core combat language:

- Basic melee exchanges
- Fast animal pressure
- Weak swarm targets
- Ranged pressure
- Persistent hazards
- Restraint/controller threats
- Heavy response-rule minibosses
- Fog and awareness pressure
- A two-phase final combat examination

## Roster

- Six standard enemies
- Two minibosses
- Keeper of the Gate

## Functional spaces

- Combat rooms
- Shrine
- Rest
- Shop
- Treasure/miniboss spaces
- Boss approach and old gate arena

## Production relationship

Milestone 1 establishes the base Hushiro combat kit and three enemies. Milestone 2 completes the remaining region art, shared Shrine foundation, room skins, minibosses, and boss.
