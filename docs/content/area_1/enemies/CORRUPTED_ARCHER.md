# Corrupted Archer

## Gameplay role

Baseline ranged-pressure enemy for Area 1. He forces movement, punishes passive or straight-line approaches, and becomes vulnerable when the player closes distance cleanly.

## One-sentence fantasy

A corrupted garrison archer whose body still remembers firing lines and distance discipline, retreating from close pressure so he can keep killing from safety.

## Lore context

These were Hushiro's watch-position and firing-line defenders, trained to hold towers, barricades, and open approaches behind the frontline. Corruption hollowed them out without erasing doctrine: take position, draw properly, preserve distance, and withdraw when caught too close.

## Visual identity

The Archer must remain clearly part of the same garrison family as the Corrupted Swordsman:

- dull red-and-black armor,
- worn cloth and damaged helmet,
- pallid grey skin,
- dead military bearing,
- longbow and quiver,
- lighter front-facing armor and practical ranged-duty gear.

## Silhouette

Leaner and more extended than the Swordsman. The long bow, quiver, drawn-arm shape, and aiming posture must communicate ranged duty immediately without making him look elegant, acrobatic, or culturally separate from the garrison.

## Weapon and attack language

- readable nock, draw, aim, and release,
- standard disciplined arrow shot,
- aimed lane pressure from safe spacing,
- reacquisition after movement,
- retreat or disengage when the player enters close range.

He should not use trick shots or flashy acrobatics. His identity is maintaining and re-establishing a firing line.

## Corruption language

Pallid flesh, dim eyes, dried blood in armor seams, and trained spacing behavior preserved beyond conscious judgment. The disturbing quality is that battlefield distance instinct survived better than the person.

## Personality in motion

Measured at range, immediately doctrinal under pressure. He gives ground without emotional panic, seeks a safe angle, and resumes fire. His behavior should read: hold line, aim, reposition, avoid entrapment, fire again.

## Combat readability

The player should instantly recognize:

- the bow draw and firing release,
- the current aim direction,
- retreat behavior,
- close-range vulnerability,
- priority-target status in mixed encounters.

## Required animation set

- patrol/position idle,
- walk,
- alert,
- aim and draw,
- release/shoot,
- reacquire target,
- retreat/flee/reposition,
- weak close-range block or reaction if supported,
- hurt,
- stagger,
- deathblow-ready,
- deathblow,
- death.

## Technical notes

Maintain strong visual continuity with the Swordsman while differentiating through bow silhouette, aiming poses, and spacing logic. Projectile origin, travel, impact, and any deflection/parry response must be readable at gameplay scale. The Archer is the Area 1 ranged-fundamentals teacher and should feel dangerous through encounter placement rather than inflated durability.
