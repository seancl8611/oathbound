# Cellar Bilemass

## Gameplay role

Area-denial support enemy. It shrinks safe footing through bile puddles that apply slow and damage-over-time while other enemies pressure the player.

## One-sentence fantasy

A crawling mass of pooled Beast Blood that learned to move, sloughing its own corrosive body across the battlefield.

## Lore context

Beast Blood runoff collects in cellars, storehouses, crawlspaces, and flooded corners beneath Hushiro. When enough residue coagulates, it begins to move toward life. The Bilemass has no former human or animal identity; it is hostless corruption behaving like an organism.

## Visual identity

- low amorphous mound of red-black flesh-sludge,
- glossy wet surface,
- sagging unstable volume,
- yellow-green bile escaping from a split cavity,
- no proper face or anatomy,
- trailing sludge edges and constant drip behavior.

## Silhouette

Low, squat, broad, and mound-like. It should read immediately as a ground-based hazard creature, distinct from every upright Area 1 unit.

## Attack language

- body swell or convulsion before release,
- short arcing bile spit or vomit,
- persistent hazardous puddle,
- slow crawl or lurch to change position.

Its danger is cumulative space corruption rather than direct melee lethality.

## Corruption language

Coagulated tissue, adhesive blood-sludge, bile sheen, pulsing lumps, and barely organized movement. Avoid recognizable humanoid anatomy or an overdesigned monster face.

## Personality in motion

Slow, wet, inertial, and unpleasantly purposeful. It gathers, sags, lurches, wobbles, and reforms. Spitting should visibly shift the mass's volume before it collapses back into a resting wobble.

## Combat readability

The player should recognize it as a kill-priority support threat: not fast or durable, but increasingly dangerous if left alive. The spit telegraph and puddle boundary must remain obvious beneath mixed combat effects.

## Required animation set

- idle wet wobble,
- crawl/lurch locomotion,
- vomit/spit windup,
- vomit/spit release,
- hurt,
- stagger if used,
- deathblow-ready and deathblow only if the shared combat system requires them,
- collapse/splatter death.

## Technical notes

This should be a production-efficient enemy with a small base animation library and strong material animation. The puddle is the primary gameplay output and requires separate VFX with clear edges, duration feedback, and value/color separation from mud, blood stains, and ordinary wet ground.
