# Kagutsuchi Court

Kagutsuchi Court is Area 3 and the endgame region. It represents formal authority, elite martial preservation, ritual systems, hollow vessels, and the Shogun's refusal to release the island.

## Current standard enemies

- Court Guard
- Court Caster
- Elite Defender
- Hollow Vessel
- Court Sentinel

## Current major encounters

- Blood Lotus encounter
- Eternal Swordsman
- Eclipse Shogun

The exact classification of the Blood Lotus and Eternal Swordsman as minibosses, bosses, or multi-stage encounters remains to be confirmed.

## Combat identity

- Dense elite melee pressure
- Court projectiles and ritual zones
- Revive or vessel mechanics
- Defender/support relationships
- Frenzy or escalation states
- Multi-phase final-boss rules

## Environment identity

Formal court architecture, ritual spaces, preserved authority, blood-lit seams, ceremonial defense, and increasingly direct proximity to the Wellspring.

## Production warning

The Eclipse Shogun should be treated as a dedicated internal batch or set of batches inside Milestone 6. Final-boss concept, phases, arena, Wellspring effects, UI, and ending presentation must be developed as one dependency chain.
