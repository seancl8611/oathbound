# Court Sentinel

## Gameplay role

Heavy Area 3 bruiser and space-control enforcer. A slow, high-commitment melee threat that dominates nearby space with crushing kanabō swings, punishes greed hard, and shifts into a dangerous slam-frenzy phase at low health.

## One-sentence fantasy

A towering ceremonial guardian preserved in immaculate court armor, crushing intruders with solemn judgment before erupting into a final burst of unstable violence.

## Lore context

Court Sentinels were once the inner sanctum’s most trusted executioners, temple guardians, and oath-burdened punishers — retainers tasked with carrying out physical judgment inside Kagutsuchi Court. They were instruments of sanctioned force: the ones called when discipline had to become pain. In the preserved perfection of the Court, their role hardened rather than vanished. Bound in ceremonial armor and sustained by Beast Blood, they continue as living monuments of restraint and punishment. When pushed toward defeat, the control fractures and contained violence breaks loose in a brief destructive frenzy.

## Visual identity

A massive inner-court guardian in pristine ceremonial plate, nearly twice the height and mass of an ordinary soldier. The armor should feel heavy, formal, and prestigious rather than crude: clean black-iron surfaces, deep crimson lacquer panels, gold trim, bound prayer strips, and a broad flat kanabō carried like a ritual instrument of judgment. During the low-health frenzy, red Beast Blood cracks glow through armor seams and prayer strips ignite or flare.

## Silhouette

Huge, broad, and unmistakably dominant. The key silhouette is a towering armored figure with thick torso mass, oversized shoulders, a wide planted stance, and a heavy kanabō extending the threat line across the screen. Prayer strips and a horned helmet or crest may add secondary detail, but the primary read must remain massive armored bruiser.

## Weapon / attack language

The Sentinel uses slow, crushing kanabō control: large sweeps, overhead crushes, and space-denial slams with long commitment and punishing payoff. At low health, restraint breaks and it chains repeated ground slams or violent smash patterns in rapid succession. This frenzy should be managed through spacing, evasion, and patience until it burns out rather than challenged recklessly.

## Corruption details

In its composed phase the Sentinel looks almost perfect — immaculate armor, formal posture, little visible bodily decay, and only faint seam glow hinting at pressure beneath the plate. In frenzy, red fissures spread through the armor, heat or blood light escapes from within, and ceremonial prayer strips ignite as ritual containment fails under strain.

## Personality in motion

The normal state is solemn, slow, heavy, composed, and certain. Every step and swing feels intentional; it advances like an execution. Frenzy replaces grace with explosive force while preserving weight. The contrast between ceremonial restraint and violent breakdown is central to the enemy.

## Combat read

Windups must clearly communicate massive damage and punish greed. The frenzy transition must be unmistakable so the player recognizes that the rhythm has changed. Intended read: slow but punishing juggernaut, fair if respected, lethal if rushed, with a dangerous low-health burst state that rewards discipline.

## Required animations

Idle, walk, arc_windup, arc_swing, overhead_windup, overhead_swing, rush_windup, rush_charge, shove_windup, shove_swing, frenzy_roar, frenzy_slam, frenzy_exhausted, parried, stagger, hurt, block, deathblow, death

## Technical notes

Normal attacks need strong anticipation, heavy follow-through, and clear impact. Frenzy requires a dedicated transition through glowing armor cracks, igniting prayer strips, and a more violent posture. Likely production states include idle, heavy walk, ready stance, wide swing, overhead smash, ground slam, hurt, stagger, parried heavy recoil, death, frenzy_transition, rapid slam loop, and frenzy_end recovery. Large, dense poses must remain uncluttered against rich Court backgrounds.
