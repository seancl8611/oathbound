# The Strand

The Strand is Oathbound's persistent hub and the place Akio returns to after death.

## Functions

- Run preparation and departure
- Persistent progression
- NPC dialogue and relationship development
- Training and trials
- Discovery/codex review
- Equipment, Forge, Bloodwell, and merchant services
- Blood Aspect reflection or selection through the Blood Mirror

## Spatial identity

A coastal threshold separated from the island's main regions but still affected by its atmosphere. Docks, cliff paths, weathered structures, ritual stations, and the Blood Cavern should create a space that feels safe enough to recover without becoming comfortable or bright.

## Primary NPCs

- Keeper
- Peddler
- Smith
- Raven
- Undead Samurai
- Scribe

## Navigation requirement

Each service must be identifiable through silhouette, station design, landmark placement, and lighting. The hub should remain compact enough for repeated visits while allowing visual and state changes as progression advances.
