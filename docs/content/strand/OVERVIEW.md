# The Strand

The Strand is Oathbound's persistent hub, the controlled threshold in the island's barrier, and the place Returning Blood reconstructs Akio after failed and successful runs.

## Functions

- Run preparation and departure
- Keeper-maintained barrier passage
- Blood Aspect confirmation at the Boat
- Persistent progression
- NPC dialogue and relationship development
- Training and trials
- Discovery/codex review
- Forge, Bloodwell, merchant, and other hub services
- Blood Mirror Aspect unlocks, previews, trials, and small permanent upgrades

## Core fantasy

A practical Order threshold station built around an ancient containment anchor on a cold shoreline: black sand, salt, lantern warmth, worn wood, old ritual function, and quiet dread.

The Strand is stable enough to recover in, but it exists only because generations of the Order have maintained the barrier and prepared for the next Blood Moon crossing. Sacred threshold structures should feel older and more permanent than the working outpost built around them.

## Barrier role

The Strand was established at the barrier's one controlled point of passage.

During the Blood Moon, the Heart's increased activity strains the containment. The Keeper stabilizes a narrow route through the threshold, and the Order's sealed Boat carries one prepared warrior toward the island.

The Keeper is bound to the threshold but does not power the entire barrier alone. Order maintenance, inherited rites, the anchor structure, and his spiritual bond work together to preserve the passage.

The campaign remains beneath the Blood Moon without defining how much ordinary time passes between Akio's repeated departures.

## Palette

- Rain-dark timber
- Charcoal stone and wet shoreline rock
- Faded indigo banners
- Parchment-tan paper
- Muted moss and coastal grass
- Cold blue-gray sea
- Indigo-violet coastal mist
- Restrained lantern gold, lit-window warmth, and forge embers
- Distant crimson eclipse and blood-red accents associated with the island
- Subtle pale or muted ritual light around the barrier anchor

## Materials

Salt-worn planks, rough beams, soot-stained forge surfaces, old rope, faded cloth, brittle paper, weathered stone, cliff rock, shrine-aged markers, barrier seals, and repeatedly repaired ritual bindings. Structures are maintained but heavily exposed to spray, wind, smoke, and repeated use.

## Architecture and landmarks

The Strand should use a few distinct role-specific structures rather than repeated homes:

- dock and departure Boat,
- barrier anchor or threshold shrine aligned with the offshore passage,
- open-sided Forge,
- formal Order house,
- Keeper building or station near the threshold,
- Merchant lean-to/stall,
- Discovery Board and Scribe study,
- central Bloodwell shrine marker,
- cliff stair access,
- Blood Cavern entrance,
- offshore torii aligned with the cursed island.

The Keeper, barrier anchor, Boat, and offshore torii should read as one connected departure composition without requiring a large cinematic sequence.

## Ambient pressure

- Sea mist and drifting fog
- Wave wash
- Lantern flicker
- Forge smoke and ember drift
- Fluttering banners and paper strips
- Low coastal wind
- Restrained movement in barrier seals and threshold mist
- Distant crimson pulse or eclipse light from the island during the campaign

## Movement philosophy

NPC motion is restrained, functional, and watchful. The Strand should not feel socially bustling. Much of its life comes from environmental movement—smoke, tide, cloth, paper, light, wind, and the quiet pressure of the barrier—while characters perform maintenance, ritual, recordkeeping, and preparation.

Every service must be identifiable through silhouette, station design, landmark placement, lighting, and ambient behavior. The hub should remain compact enough for repeated visits while allowing visual and state changes as progression advances.
