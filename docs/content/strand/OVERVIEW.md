# The Strand

The Strand is Oathbound's persistent hub, the last threshold before the island, and the place Returning Blood reconstructs Akio after failed and successful runs.

## Functions

- Run preparation and departure
- Blood Aspect confirmation at the Boat
- Persistent progression
- NPC dialogue and relationship development
- Training and trials
- Discovery/codex review
- Forge, Bloodwell, merchant, and other hub services
- Blood Mirror Aspect unlocks, previews, trials, and small permanent upgrades

## Core fantasy

A practical Order threshold station built against a cold shoreline: black sand, salt, lantern warmth, worn wood, old ritual function, and quiet dread. It is stable enough to recover in, but never comfortable or free from the island's pressure.

## Palette

- Rain-dark timber
- Charcoal stone and wet shoreline rock
- Faded indigo banners
- Parchment-tan paper
- Muted moss and coastal grass
- Cold blue-gray sea
- Indigo-violet mist
- Restrained lantern gold, lit-window warmth, and forge embers
- Distant crimson eclipse and blood-red accents associated with the island and Mistveil

## Materials

Salt-worn planks, rough beams, soot-stained forge surfaces, old rope, faded cloth, brittle paper, weathered stone, cliff rock, and shrine-aged markers. Structures are maintained but heavily exposed to spray, wind, smoke, and repeated use. Sacred elements should feel older than the outpost built around them.

## Architecture and landmarks

The Strand should use a few distinct role-specific structures rather than repeated homes:

- dock and departure Boat,
- open-sided Forge,
- formal Order house,
- Keeper or scholar building,
- Merchant lean-to/stall,
- Discovery Board and Scribe study,
- central Bloodwell shrine marker,
- cliff stair access,
- Blood Cavern entrance,
- offshore torii aligned with the cursed island.

## Ambient pressure

- Sea mist and drifting fog
- Wave wash
- Lantern flicker
- Forge smoke and ember drift
- Fluttering banners and paper strips
- Low coastal wind
- Distant red-veined motion in the Mistveil

## Movement philosophy

NPC motion is restrained, functional, and watchful. The Strand should not feel socially bustling. Much of its life comes from environmental movement—smoke, tide, cloth, paper, light, and wind—while characters repeat maintenance, ritual, recordkeeping, and preparation routines.

## Navigation requirement

Every service must be identifiable through silhouette, station design, landmark placement, lighting, and ambient behavior. The hub should remain compact enough for repeated visits while allowing visual and state changes as progression advances.
