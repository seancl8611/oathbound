# Strand Interactibles

Each Strand service should read as a physical location first, a gameplay function second, and a menu or progression system third.

| Interactible | System role | Detailed source |
|---|---|---|
| Boat | Run start, crossing, and final loadout confirmation | [Boat](interactibles/BOAT.md) |
| Forge Bench | Permanent Prosthetic and Relic progression / management | [Forge Bench](interactibles/FORGE_BENCH.md) |
| Merchant Stall | Stock-based purchases and salvaged goods | [Merchant Stall](interactibles/MERCHANT_STALL.md) |
| Discovery Board | Codex, discovery tracking, and practical knowledge archive | [Discovery Board](interactibles/DISCOVERY_BOARD.md) |

## Approved upgrade-station ownership

Oathbound uses three permanent upgrade stations at launch scope:

- **Bloodwell:** 10 **Akio** nodes + 8 **Run Infrastructure** nodes.
- **Forge Bench:** 19 **Prosthetic** upgrades + **Relics** with Base / Mastery I / Mastery II progression.
- **Blood Mirror:** 3 **Blood Aspect** nodes per Aspect / 9 total after the first Keeper defeat.

No separate permanent upgrade station is approved for Techniques, the Merchant, the Discovery Board, Rest rooms, Shrines, rewards, or Relics. Run-support improvements belong under the Bloodwell's single **Run Infrastructure** umbrella.

## System ownership boundary

- **Boat:** final run-start confirmation; it does not own permanent progression.
- **Shrine:** handles in-run Resist / Embrace / Tier growth.
- **Blood Mirror:** permanent Aspect progression, trials, previews, and related mastery content after first Keeper.
- **Bloodwell:** Akio + Run Infrastructure permanent progression and the Strand-side revival anchor.
- **Forge Bench:** Prosthetic + Relic permanent progression and Strand-side management.
- **Blood Cavern:** provides the physical training and trial space rather than a separate general upgrade tree.
- **Discovery Board:** codex/discovery progression rather than a permanent power tree.
- **Merchant Stall:** purchasing/service role rather than a permanent upgrade tree unless explicitly expanded later.

These functions must not collapse into one generic upgrade screen.

## Opening availability

- The **Bloodwell opens after Akio's first return**.
- The **Blood Mirror unlocks after the first Keeper defeat**.
- Blood Mirror Node 2 becomes available after first Twin Maws; Node 3 after first Shogun / first Binding clear.
- The outer Blood Cavern training space may function before the Mirror unlocks.

Other service unlock timing may still be staged for onboarding where it does not alter launch production scope.

## Shared rules

- Stations must remain recognizable without opening their menus.
- UI should feel grounded in the station's materials and function.
- Locked, available, selected, purchased, completed, mastered, and unavailable states must be clear where applicable.
- Final interface art should follow the documented data and progression structure.
- Repeated use must remain fast enough for the roguelite return loop.
