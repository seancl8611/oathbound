# Merchant Stall

## Strand function

The Merchant Stall is the purchase point for run-support items, materials, charms, fragments, and occasional unusual stock.

## Lore role

The Peddler's tide-line stall is stocked with lacquer fragments, relics, prosthetic parts, rubble, prayer beads, sealed scraps, and other objects recovered from the island's ruin.

## Visual identity

- Canvas cover
- Low shelves
- Wrapped goods and item bundles
- Dangling tags
- Weathered boxes
- Candle or lantern warmth
- Curated salvage rather than a clean storefront

## Interaction fantasy

The player shops from someone who turns the island's ruin into usable inventory. The experience should feel useful, grounded, and slightly suspect.

## Screen behavior

Use a fast list or grid with:

- item card or icon,
- item type/category,
- rarity marker where applicable,
- price,
- stock count,
- short effect text,
- player currency,
- owned state where relevant,
- sold-out state,
- support for limited stock and a future reroll system without requiring it now.

## Presentation goal

Item cards may feel like tagged salvage arranged on cloth rather than sterile RPG inventory slots.

## Animation and environment needs

- cloth sway,
- candle/lantern flicker,
- bead or tag movement,
- Peddler object-handling loops,
- subtle stock movement that reinforces the stall's layered identity.

## Technical notes

- Prioritize speed and legibility over ornamental framing.
- Reuse consistent icons across items, relics, materials, currencies, rarity, and purchase state.
- The physical stock spread and screen should share the same category language.
