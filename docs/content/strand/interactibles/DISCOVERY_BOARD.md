# Discovery Board

## Strand function

The Discovery Board is the codex and unlock archive for enemies, prosthetics, relic discoveries, item notes, regional knowledge, and fragments of island history.

## Lore role

The Scribe physically maintains the Board outside her study, pinning pages that document weaknesses, schematics, recovered evidence, and incomplete conclusions.

## Visual identity

- Pinned and layered papers
- Weighted corners
- String ties
- Brittle notes
- Hand-drawn diagrams and weak-point sketches
- Margin annotations
- Paper visibly affected by coastal wind

## Interaction fantasy

The player reviews what the Order has learned rather than opening a generic encyclopedia. Knowledge is physical, incomplete, revised, and maintained by a person.

## Screen behavior

Use scalable tabs or categories for:

- Enemies
- Prosthetics
- Relics
- Items
- Lore / Records
- Areas, if later needed

Each entry should support:

- locked,
- partially discovered,
- complete,
- art plate or icon,
- lore summary,
- gameplay notes,
- discovery progress,
- related entries where useful.

## Presentation goal

Paper-based archival intelligence. The interface should feel like an organized extension of the Scribe's physical records, not a clean digital database.

## Animation and environment needs

- paper flutter,
- page lift in wind,
- stone or metal paperweights,
- nearby study glow,
- Scribe writing, sorting, and board-adjustment loops.

## Technical notes

The taxonomy must support gradual unlocks and future content without a structural rewrite. Locked and partial entries should be designed from the beginning rather than added late.
