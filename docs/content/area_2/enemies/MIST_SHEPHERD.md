# Mist Shepherd

## Gameplay role

Support buffer and encounter-escalation enemy. Its flute song agitates the Beast Blood already present in nearby creatures, increasing aggression and pressure until the player finds and removes it.

## One-sentence fantasy

A drifting flute spirit whose mournful song rouses the Beast Blood in nearby creatures, making the whole encounter more violent until the music is silenced.

## Lore context

The Shepherd is a ceremonial or shrine-bound spirit whose old purpose has been overwritten. Its music no longer guides rites or mourning; it resonates with hunger, territorial rage, and unfinished pursuit in the grove's corrupted life and dead remnants.

## Visual identity

- tall thin hooded spirit,
- tattered ceremonial robes,
- slightly floating body with lower hem dissolving into mist,
- flute raised to a shadowed or absent face,
- hanging ritual cords,
- thin mist threads or pulse wisps linking to affected enemies.

## Silhouette

Strong vertical robe shape with a distinctive horizontal flute pose. It must read as a ritual support spirit, not a melee wraith or generic caster.

## Attack and support language

The song is the primary weapon. Its core outputs are:

- visible buff links to affected enemies,
- pulse rhythm synchronized with the flute,
- repositioning or teleport after a brief break in sound,
- optional minor close-range spirit pulse or defensive mist flare.

Direct damage should remain secondary to encounter escalation.

## Corruption language

Ritual function hollowed into hostile resonance. The void beneath the hood, spirit-thinned body, mist-dissolving robe, and effect on nearby Beast Blood are more important than gore or physical mutation.

## Personality in motion

Calm, distant, and ceremonial. It drifts, maintains form, and relocates as if the song itself moved. Damage should briefly break musical control rather than provoke exaggerated physical recoil.

## Combat readability

The player should often hear the Shepherd before seeing it. Flute posture, distinctive audio, and mist links must clearly communicate that it is amplifying the encounter. Its death should create an obvious silence and release from the buff state.

## Required animation set

- idle/flute loop,
- drifting movement,
- cast/buff pulse,
- teleport or relocation,
- hurt with musical break,
- stagger/interrupt,
- deathblow-ready,
- deathblow,
- death/dissolve.

## Technical notes

Audio-visual ownership is essential. Buff links must be visible enough to explain the relationship without cluttering mixed encounters. The base animation library can remain restrained, with most motion coming from robe drift, mist timing, flute pose, and relocation effects.
