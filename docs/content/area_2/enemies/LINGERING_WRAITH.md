# Lingering Wraith

## Gameplay role

Spectral melee duelist and timing-disruption enemy. It combines disciplined katana fundamentals with controlled flicker movement, delayed commitment, and short spiritual repositioning.

## One-sentence fantasy

A long-dead samurai spirit whose body is gone but whose combat discipline still manifests as a half-formed warrior continuing the duel after death.

## Lore context

Lingering Wraiths are warriors from different eras whose bodies failed while martial identity remained. Some are ancient samurai; others may be Order warriors lost during earlier Blood Moon crossings.

## Visual identity

- recognizable samurai helmet, armor profile, and sword stance,
- body assembled from spirit smoke, ash, and blood-mist,
- black void where the face should be,
- faint red internal light,
- incomplete translucent armor and frayed cloth edges,
- pale-white and blood-red katana trails.

The central body must remain readable as a swordsman rather than dissolving into an effects cloud.

## Silhouette

Tall, clean, disciplined samurai profile with unstable edges. Helmet, shoulders, sword, and lower-body stance remain firm while hems, ash, and smoke trail subtly.

## Weapon and attack language

- measured slash strings,
- clean thrusts,
- poised ready states,
- short phase-like repositioning,
- partial dissolve and deliberate rematerialization,
- restrained dodge or charge behavior.

The spectral layer should disrupt learned timing without making hit timing arbitrary.

## Corruption language

Spiritual persistence rather than flesh mutation: void spaces, ash-frayed body edges, blood-mist, red seam glow, and a body barely held together by martial memory.

## Personality in motion

Formal, calm, and relentless. Controlled steps and stance resets remain the foundation. Supernatural movement appears through slight flicker, smoke-thin glide, or partial dissolve rather than wild ghost behavior.

## Combat readability

The intended read is an advanced spirit duelist: refined, fair, harder to rush, and capable of changing position without losing clear attack intent. Targetability, reappearance, blade path, startup, active timing, and recovery must remain explicit.

## Required animation set

- idle,
- walk/run or controlled glide,
- slash windup and slash strings,
- thrust windup and thrust,
- charge windup and charge attack,
- dodge/flicker reposition,
- block,
- hurt,
- parried recoil,
- stagger/posture break,
- deathblow-ready,
- deathblow,
- dissolve death.

## Technical notes

Keep spectral drift and trails on supporting VFX layers where practical. Avoid excessive transparency. The katana and upper-body posture are the primary timing anchors. Spiritual instability may complicate tracking, but cannot conceal whether the Wraith is targetable, attacking, or recovering.
