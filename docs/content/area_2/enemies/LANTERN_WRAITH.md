# Lantern Wraith

## Gameplay role

Ranged spirit-pressure and area-control support enemy. It casts crescent-like spirit flame and occasional bursts from a strong lantern focal point, forcing movement and target prioritization through low-visibility spaces.

## One-sentence fantasy

A barely formed forest spirit carrying an old shrine lantern, using its light to cast disciplined arcs of spirit flame through dark hunting paths.

## Lore context

The Lantern Wraith is the remnant of a path spirit, shrine attendant, or old guidance function bound to Yomori's trail network. A light once used to mark safe passage persisted after its meaning failed and became a hostile signal.

## Visual identity

- minimal dark hooded spirit body,
- trailing shadow or robe wisps,
- no readable anatomy or face,
- dominant old shrine lantern,
- warm amber-orange glow against the cold forest,
- white-hot flare before the strongest burst,
- restrained ember and heat-distortion support effects.

## Silhouette

Narrow, floating, and iconic: hooded wraith plus hanging lantern. The body stays soft-edged and simple while the lantern remains the hard visual anchor in every pose.

## Attack language

- present or lift lantern for windup,
- crescent spirit-flame projectile,
- short lantern swing that releases a flame arc,
- radial or localized AOE after a white-hot flare,
- quiet repositioning to preserve range.

The lantern is always the attack origin and primary telegraph source.

## Corruption language

Guidance turned predatory. The body is mostly absence; corruption concentrates in the warped ritual light, hostile flame, and continued path-marking function.

## Personality in motion

Slow-drifting, composed, and watchful. It glides, pivots minimally, pauses before attacks, and feels like an omen choosing when to flare rather than a frantic caster.

## Combat readability

The player should understand that lantern brightness and movement communicate attack intent. The unit is fragile or interruptible when pressured, but dangerous if allowed to control lanes from range.

## Required animation set

- hovering idle,
- glide/walk,
- lantern lift or attack windup,
- shoot/release,
- AOE channel,
- AOE flare and burst,
- reposition/jump if used,
- hurt,
- stagger/interrupt,
- block or defensive reaction if supported,
- deathblow-ready,
- deathblow,
- death/dissolve.

## Technical notes

Keep the base sprite simple and place glow, heat distortion, and ember trails on separate VFX layers. The white-hot AOE flare must remain unmistakable. In a visually ambiguous region, this enemy should improve clarity through one reliable light-source telegraph.
