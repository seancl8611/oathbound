# Area 2 Standard Enemies

## Family fantasy

Feral, spiritual, hunting-ground corruption built around predation, territoriality, unstable bodies, lingering memory, and a forest that has become saturated by long-term Beast Blood use.

## Shared motifs

- stalking and ambush,
- shrine-path trespass,
- half-seen movement between trunks,
- broken hunting routes,
- roots, mist, fungal pulse, and red sap,
- old ritual forms distorted by long corruption,
- earlier human and animal identities becoming less physically stable rather than merely gaining statistics.

## Shared material language

Bone-white bark, red-veined trunks, blood-rich sap, wet roots, corpse-dark earth, slick stone, moss-black growth, cyan fungal light, old shrine wood, cracked lanterns, rotting rope, trail markers, and swallowed hunting gear.

## Shared corruption language

Area 2 expresses Beast Blood after prolonged bodily and spiritual instability: spirit residue, feral persistence, partial manifestation, ash-frayed silhouettes, root binding, pale ghost-light, and predatory urges continuing after the original body has weakened or failed.

The exact process remains intentionally flexible. Area 2 spirits belong to the same curse but do not require a separate magic system or a rigid hierarchy beneath the Shogun.

## Movement philosophy

Enemies stalk, circle, flicker, glide, relocate, emerge from obscured angles, or use the forest to constrain paths. Even support units should feel embedded in a hostile living landscape. Uncertainty comes from positioning and manifestation, not from hiding the rules.

## Readability priorities

- targetable and untargetable states must be explicit,
- manifestation and reappearance points must be readable,
- lunge and pounce lines need clear commitment,
- buff links and control zones must show ownership,
- ambient motion must not be confused with attack startup,
- spirit VFX cannot obscure blade direction or safe space.
