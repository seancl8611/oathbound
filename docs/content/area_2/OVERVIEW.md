# Yomori Grove

Yomori Grove is Area 2, a mist-heavy region where Beast Blood expresses through spirits, lantern rites, roots, predators, fungal growth, and unstable boundaries between body and presence.

## Current standard enemies

- Lingering Wraith
- Lantern Wraith
- Mist Shepherd
- Stalker Hound

## Major encounters

The region currently includes two minibosses and a paired boss structure involving Rootfang and Briarthorn. Exact encounter names, sequence, revive/soul-transfer rules, and whether Rootfang and Briarthorn are separate phases or a duo fight must be confirmed before this file becomes approved.

## Combat identity

- Spirit manifestation and disappearance
- Mist-linked support behavior
- Predator pressure
- Root and fungal area control
- Fire-rite or lantern interactions
- Multi-entity boss coordination

## Production dependencies

- Area 1 establishes shared scale, VFX hierarchy, and room language.
- Area 2 requires its own base environment kit before final room variants.
- Spirit effects must preserve attack direction and safe-space readability.
- Duo-boss effects must communicate which entity is active, vulnerable, linked, or transferring power.
